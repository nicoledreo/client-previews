Newfoundland and Labrador Cannabis Retail Licence Overview

Newfoundland and Labrador has adopted a private retail model, and the sale of recreational cannabis is being conducted through private retailers licensed and regulated by the Newfoundland and Labrador Liquor Corporation (NLC).

The NLC maintains absolute control of the sale, distribution, delivery, and pricing of cannabis products, and operates Newfoundland and Labrador’s only legal recreational online store Cannabis NL.

There are currently 55 dispensaries in Newfoundland and Labrador, whose locations were chosen based on population and accessibility. Depending on sales, it is expected that additional Newfoundland and Labrador retail locations will open up in the near future. Prospective retailers can only apply to become a Licensed Cannabis Retailer (LCR) when the NLC issues a Request for Proposal for LCRs based on an increase in consumer demand.

In spring and summer 2024 the NLC is opening an application window for Tier 4 cannabis retailer licences in four different locations across NL. The application window date, and number of licences available is currently TBD.

The locations that will be accepting Tier 4 Retailer applications are:

  1. Norris Point to Woody Point
  2. Millertown to Buchans
  3. Old Perlican to Winterton
  4. Fogo Island

02

Tier 1 Cannabis Retailer Licence

A Tier 1 cannabis retail licence store is a standalone retail establishment dedicated to selling cannabis, with a separate exterior entrance. This licence allows you to open a typical cannabis dispensary that allows for the sale of cannabis and cannabis products. In addition to cannabis, these stores may sell non-alcoholic beverages and branded merchandise, excluding food items. Tier 1 Cannabis Stores are known for their extensive product selection and specialized service staff. This licence has an annual renewal fee of $125

Retailers with a Tier 1 license are permitted to sell cannabis online. Online products must be listed and priced by the NLC. While sales can occur at any time, all transactions must be processed, and payments received before the products are sent for delivery.

Tier 2 Cannabis Retailer Licence

A Tier 2 licence authorizes a standalone cannabis store that is co-located within a different retail establishment that is not a cannabis store, does not include a pharmacy, and has not been issued a lounge license. The area where cannabis is stored, sold, or supplied must be separate from the retail establishment, and customers should not need to pass through the cannabis store to access the retail establishment or any unrelated area. This licence has a renewal fee of $125.

Tier 3 Cannabis Retailer Licence

A Tier 3 cannabis retail licence may be issued for a separate point of sale within a retail establishment that does not include a pharmacy and has not been issued a lounge license under the Liquor Licensing Regulations. This means that there is a separate, distinct counter where cannabis is sold. This does not mean that a stand-alone dispensary can be opened on the same premise as the retail store hosting the cannabis establishment. this licence has a renewal fee of $125.

Tier 4 Cannabis Retailer Licence

A Tier 4 Cannabis retail location is a convenience-focused store selling cannabis and accessories from the primary retail counter. Businesses like convenience stores can get this license and sell cannabis and cannabis products at their primary retail counter, comparable to the sale of tobacco. This does not permit the opening of a stand-alone cannabis dispensary. This licence has an annual renewal fee of $125.

06

How to Apply for a Retail Cannabis License in Newfoundland

The application fee for a cannabis retailer licence in Newfoundland is $125.

to apply for a retail cannabis license in Newfoundland, you will need to provide personal data, proof of identity and other information about the individual proposed to be in charge of the premises. Applicants must also obtain written confirmation from the city or municipality where the premises are located. This confirmation should state that there are no objections to issuing the license and that the proposed cannabis store complies with local zoning by-laws and cannabis regulations.

This means that applicants will need to own or hold a valid lease agreement stating that they are occupying the premises in order to apply for a license. The extent of this requirement is dictated by the retailer licence tier.

Site Plan

A detailed, scaled plan of the premises must be submitted, showing all areas and dimensions. For Tier 3 or Tier 4 licenses, a plan of the retail establishment where the cannabis store will be located is also required.

Proof must be provided that all exterior windows of the cannabis store are either hardened or protected against breaking or are not easily accessible if broken. Evidence is also required that all entrance and exit doors are hardened or protected and equipped with commercial locks or locking mechanisms. The application will also require plans displaying that the premise has adequate lighting and meets all cannabis regulations set in Newfoundland.

Additional Information

Evidence must be provided that the applicant is not employed by the NLLC, is not a board member, is not a police officer, and is not indebted to the NLLC or the board. A signed consent form for the disclosure of information related to the application must be included.

Application Process

  1. Gather all required documents and information as specified above.

  2. Complete the application form with accurate and up-to-date information.

  3. Submit the application along with all supporting documents to the appropriate licensing board.

  4. Await confirmation and any further instructions from the board regarding the status of your application.

Ensure that all information provided is accurate and complete to avoid delays in the processing of your application.
