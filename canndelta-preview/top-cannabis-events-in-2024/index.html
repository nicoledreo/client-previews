Top Cannabis Events in 2024!

Feb 28, 2024Grace Sullivan7 min read

Why you Need to Attend Cannabis Industry Events!

2024 is jam-packed with cannabis industry events all over North America. By actively participating in events, CannDelta has the chance to express our passion for the cannabis industry and share it with entrepreneurs and enthusiasts who are equally excited! We prioritize being present at as many events as possible in hopes to continue educating communities and aspiring business owners about the benefits and opportunities cannabis presents.

Attending cannabis business conferences and events is one of the best ways to learn more about your community and fast-track your cannabis business development. Events are an easy way to gain a better understanding of the current state of your cannabis market and learn more about trending innovations all while meeting potential clients, partners, leaders, and enthusiasts! Get invaluable feedback from others who have been in your shoes and find out what it’s going to take to be successful in Cannabis.

CannDelta often gives out free event tickets through our social media channels! Don’t miss your chance to capitalize on this opportunity! Connect with us on socials below for a chance to win tickets and learn more about cannabis events near you.

CannDelta Socials

Business of Cannabis Toronto – Leadership Summit, ON

Niagara Street, Toronto, ON.

This Canadian event is a hub for cannabis professionals looking to dive into the current state of the Canadian cannabis market. By hosting 200 cannabis business leaders, this event will be packed with great ideas and great information about the future of Canada’s cannabis market. If you’re a current or aspiring Canadian cannabis business owner, this is a cannabis business conference you won’t want to miss! The summit will feature talks and debates from industry leaders and policy experts, two networking sessions, Canadian Cannabis Report insights, and lots more! You’ll be able to spot CannDelta’s booth and watch our CEO Dr. Sherry Boodram speak about Cannabis in Toronto! The event has been postponed to the end of 2024. Check back frequently for the specific date!

Get Tickets Here!

The Emerald Conference San Diego, CA

Loews Coronado Bay, San Diego, CA.

This cannabis industry event is the longest-running, science-focused gathering dedicated to cannabis and psychedelics! You’ll learn about all the latest research, innovations, and possibilities surrounding cannabis cultivation, processing, and product development. This event’s for you if you’re interested in, or are a professional in analytical testing, extraction science, cannabis & psychedelic research and development, cultivation science, psychedelics, and everything in between. Hundreds of attendees from both the U.S. and international markets will be present, alongside over 22 sessions featuring more than 25 industry-leading speakers, supported by 45 sponsors. There will be over 25 technical poster presentations and presenters, with networking breakfasts, luncheons, and receptions all included in your ticket. CannDelta’s background in scientific compliance will make our booth one you won’t want to miss if you’ll be at this event. Our team is loaded with experts that have a sincere love for the crossover of science and cannabis! This event is taking place from April 1 to 3 at Loews Coronado Bay, San Diego, CA.

Get Tickets Here!

COVA Cannabis Seminar Event

Maritime Conference Center, Baltimore, MD

CannDelta’s CEO, Dr. Sherry Boodram, and CSO Dr. Lucas McCann, will be speaking at COVA’s cannabis event on April 3, 2024. This event is free to attend, making it a great opportunity for anyone interested in learning more about what it takes so start a cannabis business in Maryland. The event will host industry professionals in licensing, security, HR, employment, point-of-sales and more! If you’re searching for initial startup advice or detailed operational feedback, the seminars happening at this event will offer valuable insights for anyone interested in owning a dispensary in Maryland. The event will take place at the Maritime Conference Center in Baltimore from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Attendees will have the opportunity to hear from CannDelta’s industry leaders on topics relating to launching a compliant cannabis business in Maryland.

Get Tickets Here!

CannConnect, Minneapolis, MN

Modest Brewing Private Event Space, Minneapolis, MN.

CannaConnect is a MN based cannabis organization with the goal of growing the industry by focusing on inclusivity, education, and community engagement. The “Blazing Through the Minnesota Cannabis Industry” event is a speed networking event for cannabis professionals, prospective license holders, cannabis businesses, investors, and those interested in the cannabis sector. Whether you’re just learning about cannabis or a professional in the industry, this event offers a low-pressure environment ideal for networking and learning more about what cannabis can offer you! You’ll be able to check out the CannDelta booth to see why cannabis consulting is the next step in your business’ development! The event is happening from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM on April 25 at the Modest Brewing Private Event Space, Minneapolis, MN.

Lucky Leaf, Minneapolis, MN

Minneapolis Convention Center, Minneapolis, MN.

Lucky Leaf Expo Minneapolis is essential for cannabis professionals and job seekers in Minnesota. With Minnesota’s medical program already attracting significant investments, attendees can expect access to over 100 exhibitors, diverse speakers, and comprehensive insights. This event offers a unique chance to get an inside look at MN’s up-and-coming cannabis sector from its early stages. Following last year’s successful turnout, Lucky Leaf hopes to make this year’s event even better by including new exhibitors, speakers, and attendees. Don’t miss the opportunity to network with industry leaders like us at CannDelta and discover MN’s cannabis opportunities! CannDelta’s booth will be a great spot for you to learn more about what it will take to start your cannabis business, and what we can do to help! Lucky Leaf is taking place on April 26 and 27 between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM at the Minneapolis Convention Center!

Get Tickets Here!

NECANN Baltimore, MD

Baltimore Convention Center, Baltimore, MD

NECANN’s Cannabis Convention & Expo in Baltimore is NECANN’s first-ever cannabis event held in Maryland. This event is all about getting cannabis dispensary owners, industry professionals, cannabis consumers, and any other kind of cannabis enthusiast all in one place. By doing so, NECANN is sparking innovation and growth by giving industry leaders, brands, businesses, and enthusiasts the space to educate anyone interested in learning more about cannabis in Maryland this year! You’ll be able to find CannDelta’s booth where you can meet some of Baltimore’s top cannabis experts and learn about Maryland’s rapidly growing and promising cannabis industry! Join us at NECANN’s Cannabis Convention & Expo in Baltimore Maryland on May 3 and 4 from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM!

Get Tickets Here!

CWCB Expo New York

Javits Convention Center, New York City

CWCB is a business-to-business tradeshow for legal cannabis businesses in New York. Learn more about New York’s expanding cannabis market by taking part in educational sessions, interacting with other brands, industry professionals, and so much more. This is a big opportunity for cannabis business owners looking to make valuable connections and become better known in New York’s cannabis industry while it’s still relatively young. All in all, the CWCBExpo aims to be a space for dispensary owners, growers, suppliers, investors, legal entities, and everyone else involved in NY’s cannabis industry to interact. You’ll see CannDelta at this event representing our home state! Be sure to capitalize on the chance to connect with New York’s #1 scientific and regulatory consulting firm to learn about starting or raising your cannabis business to the next level! You can attend this Expo from June 5 to 6 at the Javits Convention Center in New York City.

Get Ticket Here!

CannaCon Rochester, NY & CannaCon Cleveland, OH

Joseph A. Floreano Rochester Riverside Convention Center, Rochester, NY

Rochester, New York

This is an event you won’t want to miss! CannCon is known as a leading cannabis expo, famous for being a hub hosting cannabis businesses and leaders from all over the globe. CannaCon’s Rochester event is all about community involvement and responsible education about New York’s cannabis sector. CannaCon is welcoming anyone and everyone that wants to learn more about cannabis and cannabis business from industry professionals, investors, brands, and more! Network and connect with New York’s industry leaders by visiting CannDelta’s booth! This event is happening at Joseph A. Floreano Rochester Riverside Convention Center on June 20 and 21 from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Get Ticket Here!

Cleveland, Ohio

You’ll see CannDelta at the Midwest Cleveland, OH, CannaCon happening on October 3 & 4 between 10:00 AM & 5:00 PM

Ohio’s on the brink of huge growth surrounding their cannabis market. This CannaCon Cleveland is all about expanding and developing Ohio’s cannabis market. This event is shaping up to be a huge opportunity for people looking to start a cannabis business in Ohio and eager to meet key players in the industry. Whether you’re looking for professional services, Ohio cannabis connections, or even opportunities for funding, CannaCon Ohio is the place to be.
