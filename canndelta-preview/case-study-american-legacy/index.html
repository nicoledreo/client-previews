Building a Legacy From The Ground Up

Brand
American Legacy
Location

The Vision

It began with the idea of creating cannabis that feels authentic, premium, and lasting. Founded by Sherman Stovall, American Legacy is more than a business; it is a movement to redefine what cannabis can represent. With a focus on cultural pride, and an unwavering commitment to quality, these products set a new standard for trust and excellence in the industry.

Strategic Planning and Brand Development

American Legacy’s journey began with CannDelta providing hands-on support to transform an idea into a fully realized cannabis brand. Our team streamlined the facility buildout, designed compliant operations, and built a premium identity ready for launch in New York’s regulated market.

Founder Sherman Stovall first considered developing the warehouse he leased in Colonie, New York. Unsure where to begin, he turned to CannDelta for expertise in licensing, operations, and branding. Together, we designed the facility layout, recommended equipment, and built the foundation of a lasting brand.

With CannDelta’s support, American Legacy launched with a complete brand identity, including logo, brandbook, website, packaging, and marketing. By 2025, it has grown into one of the nation’s most recognizable cannabis brands, trusted by its audience and celebrated for quality.

Building the Facility & Finalizing the Brand

By guiding each step of the facility buildout, we helped create a compliant, efficient operation designed to scale smoothly in New York’s regulated cannabis market.

Developed an efficient, compliant distribution facility layout that maximized workflow and security while meeting state requirements.

Coordinated contractors, managed timelines, and ensured the buildout stayed on schedule and within budget.

Introduced vetted vendors for security, equipment, and specialized services to support long-term operational success.

Aligned every detail of the buildout with New York’s strict inspection criteria, paving the way for seamless approval.

Branding & Marketing

With operations in place, CannDelta led American Legacy’s market launch and digital presence. We designed packaging, built a sleek website, and developed a strong brand identity supported by targeted campaigns.

Coaching & Advisory

CannDelta provided American Legacy with strategic coaching and advisory support focused on building a strong foundation for operations and growth. This included helping the team connect with reliable vendors, contractors, and industry partners, as well as advising on key business decisions throughout the buildout and launch process.

CannDelta’s experts offered hands-on guidance to streamline coordination between partners, ensure compliance with state regulations, and maintain consistent project momentum. The advisory relationship gave American Legacy the confidence and clarity to navigate challenges effectively while staying aligned with their brand vision and operational goals.

Brand Showcase at 2025 Revelry Buyers Club NYC Tradeshow

At the 2025 Revelry Buyers Club tradeshow in Manhattan, CannDelta supported American Legacy with a complete booth setup from start to finish. We designed and coordinated the production of all branded materials, including:

  • Custom backdrops, banners, and flags
  • Flyers, packaging samples, and branded souvenirs
  • Branded tablecloths and digital display assets

Every element was carefully planned to reflect the premium image of American Legacy and ensure they made a strong and memorable impression on the show floor.

CannDelta designed a GMP-compliant processing facility with a roadmap for equipment, layout, and production lines for dried flower and pre-rolls. By streamlining design and vendor sourcing, we cut the buildout timeline by nearly 4 months and saved about $250,000 in costs.

We also developed American Legacy’s full product line and built a premium brand identity with a tailored marketing strategy, positioning the company to enter the New York market ready for both compliance and consumer recognition.

Today, American Legacy is one of New York’s fastest-rising cannabis brands, backed by a strong identity, fully compliant operations, and a pipeline of premium products.

Why It Matters

What began as a single facility buildout is now a brand with products on shelves, a digital presence that drives thousands of visits each month, and a growing consumer base across New York. By focusing on compliance, strategy, and brand identity from the start, American Legacy turned an idea into measurable growth and long-term market traction.
