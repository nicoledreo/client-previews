Learn · Medical cannabis

Medical cannabis in Minnesota

Short answer: we don't sell it. My Dealer is an adult-use store for adults 21 and older. Here's where to go instead.

OCM's Division of Medical CannabisRead the FAQ

We're an adult-use store.

Our license is for adult-use retail only. We sell to adults 21 and older with a valid ID, and we don't sell medical cannabis.

We also can't diagnose or certify anyone. Nothing on this site, and nothing said across our counter, is medical advice.

That isn't a dodge. It's what our license allows, and it's better said up front.

The program lives at OCM.

Minnesota's Medical Cannabis Program is run by the Office of Cannabis Management's Division of Medical Cannabis. For how the program works, go straight to the source: mn.gov/ocm/medical-cannabis.

Questions about the program belong with the people who run it, not with a store. We'd rather point you to the right place than guess.

Want to check who's licensed for what? OCM publishes that too, on its licensed-business list. Ours is Cannabis Retailer License DIS-L24-000241, adult-use retail only.

Talk to a provider first.

Cannabis can change how some medications work. If you have a health condition or take medication, talk with your health care provider or pharmacist first, whether you'd buy from us or from anyone else.

Medical questions deserve medical answers. A budtender is good for "what's in this?" and "how does this format work?" A provider is who you ask about your health.

What our budtenders can do.

We can talk about products, formats, aroma, and what's on the label. That covers things like:

  • What a label says: serving size, total THC, the batch number.
  • How a format works: how fast it starts and how long it lasts.
  • Minnesota's purchase limits, and where you can legally use it.

What we won't do is recommend cannabis for any condition, or promise what it will do for you.

A good retailer checks ID, answers what it can, admits what it cannot, and never promises a medical result.My Dealer, Cannabis 101

What we do sell.

Adult-use cannabis and hemp products, labeled as one or the other, to adults 21 and older with a valid ID, within Minnesota's limits. If that's what you're after, these pages cover it:

Medical questions, answered honestly.

Do you sell medical cannabis?

No. We're an adult-use store and sell to adults 21 and older only.

Can you certify me for the medical program?

No. We can't diagnose or certify anyone. Start with OCM's Division of Medical Cannabis and your health care provider.

Can your budtenders give medical advice?

No. We're not doctors, and we don't diagnose or recommend cannabis for any condition.

Are your products FDA-approved?

No. As the state-required warning says, these products are not evaluated or approved by the FDA.

General information, not medical advice. Laws and guidance can change, so check OCM for the current program details before you rely on anything here.
