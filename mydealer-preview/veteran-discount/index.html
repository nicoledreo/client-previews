Combining

It's on the poster, too

There's a framed poster in the Bayport store. The top of it says it better than we can:

15% OFF EVERY DAY
Salute! You've earned it.The veterans poster in the Bayport store

That's the whole idea. Show the ID. Get the 15%.

Hemp helped build America

Next to the poster is a 13-star reproduction flag. The poster tells you what it's made of:

Our 13-star reproduction flag beside this sign is made of hemp, the same tough fiber that rigged American ships from the Revolution on.The veterans poster, under “Hemp Helped Build America”

Stop in and take a look. The flag is real hemp. The discount is real too.

Ordering ahead? Still 15%.

The discount covers order-ahead too. Order on the live menu, then pick it up the same day or the next day. You pay when you pick up. Bring your ID, and show the military or veteran ID at checkout.

If an order isn't picked up by the end of the next day, it goes back into inventory.

What to bring

  • A valid ID showing you're 21 or older. We check every visit, no exceptions. Our FAQ lists every ID we accept.
  • A valid military or veteran ID, for the discount.
  • Cash or a debit card. Debit runs through our cashless ATM, and there's a regular ATM on site. No credit cards.

Leave anyone under 21 at home. Nobody under 21 comes in, even with a parent. Not sure your ID works? Call the store at 651-323-1266 and ask.

One discount per cart

These discounts don't stack, but don't worry, you'll get the best possible discount on your cart. That includes Andersen Tuesdays, unless a promo specifically says it stacks. If a promo beats 15% off, you get the promo.

Nearly all of our promotions are for rewards members. Every way to save is on the deals page.

Find us

243 3rd Street North, Bayport, on Highway 95 through downtown. Next door to the American Legion and the post office, across from city hall and the police station. Open noon to 8, every day. Longer hours are on the way.

Park in the lot out back: turn onto 1st Ave N, then take a left down the alley. The building has entrances at both the front and the back, so come in whichever way is easier. Directions from both sides of town are on the Bayport store page.
