Deal questions

Does the veteran discount work if I order ahead?

Yes. It's 15% off the entire order, every day, in store or order ahead, with a valid military or veteran ID. Details are on the veteran discount page.

Can I mix strains to get the bulk price?

Yes, within a price tier. Mix and match strains in the same tier, and the combined weight sets the price. As of October 22, 2026, a mixed ounce is $210 in the $11 tier and $225 in the $12 tier.

What taxes will I pay?

Adult-use cannabis products carry Minnesota's 15% cannabis gross receipts tax, plus the 6.875% state sales tax and any local sales tax where you buy. Every tax shows on your receipt.

Do you have an app?

Not yet. A My Dealer customer app is coming soon. Until then, use the live menu at mydealermn.com/bayport to browse and preorder.
