  1. 01

    ID, every visit

    You have to be 21 or older with a valid ID, and we check it every time. Any U.S. state license or ID works, and so do Canadian IDs, Tribal IDs, and U.S. passports and passport cards. No expired or altered IDs, and no foreign driver's licenses or national ID cards. That part is state law. The full ID list.

  2. 02

    Paying

    Cash
    Yes. There's a regular ATM on site.
    Debit
    Through the cashless ATM. It has a $3 fee and runs in $10 steps, so the withdrawal rounds up to the nearest $10 and we hand you the difference back in cash.
    Credit cards
    No.
    Coins
    None. After tax, totals round up to the nearest dollar, and that little bit goes to the employees as their tip.

    Every tax shows on your receipt. What they are.

Buy here. Use it at home.

There's nowhere to use it at the store. Bayport bans smoking or vaping cannabis in public places, our parking lot included, and bans open cannabis drinks on streets, sidewalks and parking areas.

Nobody can use cannabis in a vehicle, parked or moving. For the ride home, keep it in the trunk or rear cargo area unless it's still sealed in the original package. And driving high is a DWI, even if you feel fine.

One purchase tops out at 2 ounces of flower, 8 grams of concentrate (vapes count here), and 800 mg of THC across edibles, hemp edibles and hemp THC drinks. Minnesota limits and rules, in full.

Bayport questions.

What are your hours?

Noon to 8, every day. Longer hours are on the way.

Is this real cannabis or a hemp shop?

Real cannabis. We're licensed by the Minnesota Office of Cannabis Management. Our flower is grown as cannabis by licensed Minnesota cultivators and tracked by the state from seed to sale. We also carry some hemp-derived products, and those are labeled as hemp so you always know which is which.

Can I bring someone under 21 with me?

No. Nobody under 21 is allowed in the store, even with a parent or another adult.

I live in Wisconsin. Can I buy here?

Yes. Any adult 21 or older with a valid ID can buy here, including Wisconsin residents, within Minnesota's limits. Just know that what you buy is legal in Minnesota only. Cannabis isn't legal in Wisconsin, and you can't take it across the river. More for Wisconsin shoppers.

Do you sell medical cannabis?

No. We're adult-use only, for adults 21 and older, and we can't diagnose or certify anyone. Where to find Minnesota's medical program.

Do you have an app?

Not yet. A My Dealer customer app is coming soon. Until then, browse and preorder on the live menu.

Do I need to tip?

No. The roundup is the tip. Nobody here expects anything extra.

Anything else to do while I'm in Bayport?

Downtown Bayport is small and easy to walk. Brandon's short list: Not Justa Cafe, just down 3rd Street, for breakfast all day. The American Legion next door for cheese curds with a homemade blackberry barbecue dipping sauce. Woody's, a few doors down, for jalapeño poppers. The details, in his words.
