Rewards · Bayport, MN · 21+

Rewards: 3% back in points

A phone number at checkout gets you in. You earn 3% back in points on your purchases, plus member deals. Here's how it works.

Members: log in on the menuSee every way to save

3% back

How it works

  1. Join at checkout

    Give us your phone number when you pay. That signs you up.

  2. Earn points

    3% back in points on your purchases. Member deals come with it.

  3. See member deals online

    Viewing the live menu as a member? Log in to see all deals applied.

Nearly every promotion is for members

Nearly all of our promotions are for members only. Not a gimmick. It's just how we run deals here. If you plan on coming back, give us the number.

Ordering ahead? Preorder on the live menu and pick it up the same day or the next day. You pay when you pick up, and you bring your ID. Orders not picked up by the end of the next day are released back to inventory.

What to have at checkout

Your ID
21 or older, with a valid ID, every visit. No exceptions.
Your phone number
For points and member deals.
Cash or debit
Cash, or your debit card through our cashless ATM. There's a regular ATM on site too. No credit cards.
No change
We don't use coins. After tax, every total rounds up to the nearest dollar, and that little bit goes straight to the employees as their tip.

The counter is at 243 3rd Street North in Bayport, about 5 minutes south of downtown Stillwater on Highway 95. We're open noon to 8, every day. Directions and parking are on the Bayport store page. First time in? Start with your first visit.

One discount per cart

Discounts don't stack unless a promo specifically says it does. That includes the veteran and military discount and Andersen Tuesdays. You'll get the single best discount for your cart, automatically.

Every way to save

Rewards questions

How do I sign up?

Give us your phone number at checkout in the Bayport store. That's the sign-up.

What do I earn?

3% back in points on your purchases, plus member deals.

Are your deals only for members?

Nearly all of our promotions are. The veteran and military discount (15% off every day) and Andersen Tuesdays (10% off) work with a valid ID at checkout. See the veteran discount and every way to save.

How do I see member deals online?

Log in on the live menu at mydealermn.com/bayport. Members who log in see all deals applied.

Is there a rewards app?

Not yet. The My Dealer customer app is coming soon. Until then, the live menu is how you browse and preorder. More answers are in our FAQ.

What do I need to join?

Your phone number, at checkout. You'll have your ID out anyway: everyone shows a valid ID, 21+, every visit.
