Your first visit to My Dealer

First time at a dispensary, or just first time here? Here's the whole visit, start to finish. Bring your ID. Bring your questions.

Browse the live menuHours and directions

Before you go.

Bring a valid ID.

Everyone has to be 21 or older with a valid ID, every visit. Nobody under 21 can come in with you, parent or not. Any of these work:

  • A driver's license or state ID from any U.S. state
  • A Canadian driver's license or ID
  • A Tribal ID
  • A U.S. passport or passport card
  • A Minnesota instructional permit, if you're 21 or older
  • A passport, if you're a foreign national

We can't take expired IDs, altered IDs, or a foreign driver's license or national ID card (that's state law, not us being picky). The photo has to match you.

Check the hours.

Noon to 8, every day. Longer hours are on the way.

Order ahead, if you want.

Already know what you want? Preorder on the live menu and pick it up the same day or the next day. You pay at pickup, and you still bring your ID. An order that isn't picked up by the end of the next day goes back into inventory.

Find us, then park out back.

It's 243 3rd Street North, which is Highway 95 through downtown Bayport. Look for the face with the hat on the brick. Parking is easy: a paved lot behind the building, reached by turning onto 1st Ave N and taking a left down the alley. Street parking on 3rd Street and the City Hall lot across the street work too. There are doors at the front and the back, so use whichever is closer. Directions from the north and south.

At the counter, take your time.

If you're new, start with a budtender. Tell them what you're curious about. They'll walk you through labels and formats without the sales pitch.

Rather shop on your own? Use the iPad kiosk to see the live menu and build a cart, and it gets filled while you're in the store. You can also scan the QR code to bring the menu up on your own phone. If screens aren't your thing, ask for a paper menu.

Ask us anything. We'd rather slow down than guess.

A few good first questions:

  • How many milligrams are in one serving, and in the whole package?
  • What's the total THC, and what does that number mean?
  • Deli flower or a pre-pack?
  • What are the top terpenes, and how does it smell?
  • Which edibles are low-dose?

One thing budtenders won't do is give medical advice. We can talk products, formats, aroma and what's on the label, but we're not doctors. More on that.

Cash or debit. No credit cards.

Pay with cash, or with your debit card through the cashless ATM. There's a regular ATM on site too.

The cashless ATM charges $3, the same idea as a regular ATM. It works in $10 steps, so your withdrawal rounds up to the nearest $10 and you get the difference back in cash.

We don't use coins. After tax, your total rounds up to the nearest dollar, and that bit goes straight to the employees. That's the tip. Nobody expects anything extra.

Minnesota sets the limits.

A single purchase can include up to 2 ounces of flower (pre-rolls count by weight), 8 grams of concentrate (vapes count here), and 800 mg of THC in edibles, shared across cannabis edibles, hemp edibles and hemp THC drinks.

That's state law, not store policy. The Minnesota basics page covers carrying limits, serving caps, and where you can use it.

Sign up while you're here.

Give us your phone number at checkout and you're in the loyalty program. You earn 3% back in points on your purchases, and nearly all of our promotions are for members only.

Veterans and active military get 15% off every day; show a valid military or veteran ID at checkout. Andersen employees get 10% off every Tuesday with a valid Andersen ID. Discounts don't stack, but you'll get the best one your cart qualifies for. Every way to save.

After you get home.

Start low. Go slow.

If you're new, or it's been a while, start with 2.5 to 5 mg of THC or less. That's a quarter to half of a typical 10 mg gummy. Per OCM, edibles usually take 30 minutes to 2 hours to kick in. Don't take more just because you don't feel it yet. Wait at least 2 hours. Inhaled products usually start within minutes. More on edibles.

Pick one.

We don't recommend mixing cannabis and alcohol. OCM says mixing cannabis with alcohol, prescription medications, or other substances can sharply increase impairment and the risk of effects you didn't expect.

Store it right.

An airtight container in a cool, dark place. A cupboard beats a sunny windowsill. Most of all, keep it locked up in its child-resistant package. Storage, in more detail.

Keep it away from kids and pets.

Edibles and drinks can look like regular snacks, so store them apart from food. Don't smoke or vape anywhere a child would breathe it. More in the FAQ.

If a child eats cannabis, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 right away, or 911 in an emergency. If it's a pet, call your vet.

Not in the car. Not in our lot.

Nobody can use cannabis in a motor vehicle, parked or moving, driver or passenger. Our parking lot is off-limits too: Bayport's ban on smoking or vaping in public places covers it. Ride home with your purchase in the trunk or rear cargo area unless it's still sealed.

Don't drive.

Driving high is a DWI in Minnesota, even if you feel fine. Cannabis slows your reaction time and judgment. Edibles can last 6 to 12 hours. Get a ride home, or stay put until it wears off.

For the long version, OCM publishes the Minnesota OCM Guide to Safer Cannabis Use.
