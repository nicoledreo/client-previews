Deli flower or pre-packs?

Two ways to buy flower at My Dealer. One gets weighed in front of you. One comes sealed with the label already on it. Here's when each one makes sense.

See the flower menuMore on flower

Shopping3 min readBy My Dealer, Bayport

Deli flower is weighed at the counter.

Deli flower is flower we portion out for you at the counter, priced by the gram. You get exactly the amount you want instead of a fixed jar size. It's weighed to order, in front of you, on certified scales.

You can buy as little as a single gram, or as much as two ounces, which is the Minnesota limit for one purchase. The per-gram price drops as you buy more, up to a half ounce or an ounce. The deli counter carries Trawa flower, and the lineup changes as new batches come in.

Pre-packs come sealed and labeled.

Pre-packs are sealed packages, mostly 3.5 g (an eighth) and 7 g (a quarter), with the strain, the grower, and the THC numbers on the label. Some are smaller-bud "popcorn" packs. Nothing to weigh. You pick it, and it's yours.

Either way, it's the same kind of flower: grown as cannabis by licensed Minnesota cultivators and tracked by the state from seed to sale. Not the alley. Not the hemp shop.

When each one makes sense.

Go deli when

You want a specific amount, not a jar size. A single gram to try something new. A half ounce or an ounce of something you already know, where the per-gram price drops. Or a mix of strains from the same tier, weighed together.

Go pre-pack when

You want it sealed, with the strain, the grower, and the THC numbers printed on the label. An eighth or a quarter is the amount you were after anyway. You'd rather grab it and go.

Not sure which? Ask at the counter. We'd rather slow down than guess.

Deli flower comes in two price tiers. Mix and match strains within a tier, and the combined weight sets the price. Prices as of October 22, 2026.

In the $11 tier, a single gram is $11, 3.5 g works out to $10.00 a gram, 14 g to $8.57 a gram, and a full ounce to $7.50 a gram. The $12 tier steps down the same way, to $8.04 a gram at the ounce. Which strains sit in each tier changes with every batch, so check the flower menu for today's lineup.

How to ask for a gram.

  1. Check the menu

    Look over the flower menu before you come in, or pull it up on the in-store kiosk. We have paper menus too. Each strain is marked Sativa through Indica, with THC numbers and, when the lab reports them, the top terpenes.

  2. Say the weight

    Tell the budtender how much: a gram, an eighth (3.5 g), a quarter (7 g), a half ounce (14 g), or an ounce (28 g). Mixing strains? Keep them in the same tier, and the total weight counts toward the bulk price.

  3. Watch it get weighed

    It's weighed to order at the counter, right in front of you, on certified scales.

  4. Pay and go

    Bring a valid ID, every visit. We take cash, or debit through the cashless ATM. No credit cards. After tax, totals round up to the nearest dollar, and that roundup is the staff tip.

Minnesota caps one purchase at 2 ounces of flower, and pre-rolls count by weight. More on that in our Minnesota cannabis laws guide. Prefer it already rolled? See pre-rolls.

Then take it home.

Keep it in an airtight container somewhere cool and dark, locked up and away from kids and pets. In the car, it rides in the trunk unless the package is still sealed. Our storage guide has the rest.
