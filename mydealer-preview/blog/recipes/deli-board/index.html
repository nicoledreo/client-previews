The deli board

A snack board for a night in. Named after the deli counter. Nothing on it is infused, and that's the point.

Deli flower vs. pre-packsMore recipes
Makes Serves 2 to 4 adults 15 minutesNot infused

Ingredients

Method

  1. Start with the big pieces: set the cheddar and the sausage on the board first.
  2. Pile the cheese curds in the middle. They're the headliner.
  3. Fan the crackers around the edges, two kinds if you have them.
  4. Fill the gaps with pickles, fruit, and nuts.
  5. Put the mustard in a small bowl with a spoon.
  6. Set the water and glasses next to the board, and serve.

Named for the counter.

At My Dealer, deli flower is weighed at the counter and priced by the gram. This board is the other kind of deli. Cheese, sausage, crackers, pickles. No scale required.

It's for a night in, at home. Build it with what you like. The only rule is the one in the next section.

Nothing on this board is infused.

Keep it that way. Edibles can look like regular snacks, so they don't go on a snack board, next to one, or in a bowl on the coffee table. If there's anything infused in the house, it stays in its original child-resistant package, locked up and out of reach of kids and pets.

That way everyone at the table knows exactly what they're eating. And seconds on the curds are nobody's problem.

Separate means separate. Infused food stays labeled, in its own package, apart from regular food. If a child eats cannabis, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 right away, or 911 in an emergency.

Kids and pets

Cheese curds, a Minnesota classic.

The curds are the headliner. Pile them in the middle and let people find them. Squeaky is the goal.

Around them: something sharp, something salty, something pickled, something fresh. Cheddar and summer sausage cover the first two. Pickles and fruit cover the rest. Crackers hold it all together.

Make it yours.

  • No sausage? Add a second cheese, or double the pickles.
  • Want it heartier? Add rye bread or pretzels.
  • Making it ahead? Build it, cover it, and keep it in the fridge until people show up. Pull the curds out a little early. They taste better at room temperature.
  • Feeding a crowd? Make two boards instead of one big one, so nobody has to reach.

If the night includes cannabis.

Keep it simple. Cannabis or alcohol, not both. OCM says mixing cannabis with alcohol can sharply increase impairment and the risk of effects you didn't expect. Pick one.

Start low, especially with edibles: 2.5 to 5 mg of THC or less, then wait at least 2 hours. More in Edibles: start low, go slow. Use it at home, or on private property where the owner allows it, and nobody drives after.
