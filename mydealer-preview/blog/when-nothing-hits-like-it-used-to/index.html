When nothing hits like it used to

Blog · Guides · 21+

That's common with regular use. The answer usually isn't a bigger number. Here's what a lot of regulars try first.

Read the FAQ
  • Cannabis 101

Guides3 min readBy My Dealer, Bayport

It's common with regular use.

Your body adapts to THC over time, so the same amount can start to feel like less. Plenty of regulars run into it. Before trading up to stronger products, a lot of them try something simpler first.

Stronger isn't the fix.

Strong is not the same as better. A higher number means stronger, not better. THC is the easiest number to compare on a menu, but it is not a complete description of the product. Use potency to understand dose, not as a universal quality score.

Switching formats to get more is a bigger jump than it looks. Vape oil is much more concentrated than flower, so a single puff can deliver more THC than a puff of flower. Edibles take 30 minutes to 2 hours to kick in and last 6 to 12 hours.

And don't add alcohol to make it hit. OCM says mixing cannabis with alcohol, prescription medications, or other substances can sharply increase impairment and the risk of effects you didn't expect. Pick one.

Try a short break.

A lot of regulars try a short break of a few days. Put it away. Lock it up while you're at it, in its child-resistant package. Stored right, it'll be there when you get back. Our storage guide covers how.

No need for a plan more complicated than that. A few days. Then come back small.

Come back small.

When you come back, start with a small amount and work up slowly. Start like it's been a while, because it has.

  1. 01

    Flower or pre-rolls

    One inhalation, or one or two small puffs, of a lower-THC product. Then wait 15 to 30 minutes before deciding on more.

  2. 02

    Vapes

    One short, gentle puff. Then wait 15 to 30 minutes.

  3. 03

    Edibles and drinks

    2.5 to 5 mg of THC or less. Then wait at least 2 hours. Next time, take the same amount or less until you know how it goes. More in Edibles: start low, go slow.

  4. 04

    Keep notes

    The strain, the THC %, how much, and how it went. Notes beat memory.

Ask for the low end.

Coming back small is easier when the product is small too. Ask a budtender to point you to the lower end of the THC range on the flower menu, or to the low-dose edibles. Pre-rolls come in smaller joints, like 0.35 g. Pick a lower THC number. You can always light another; you can't take back what you've already smoked.

Everyone is different, and the same person can react differently from one day to the next. One more reason to start small and let the notes do the remembering.

Shop by aroma, not just THC.

Shop for terpene profiles, aroma, and flavor, not only THC %. Plenty of people find they have favorite aromas, and that's a good way to find products you like. Ask a budtender about a specific batch. Our terpenes guide has the aroma table.

When to talk to someone.

If cutting back feels hard, or cannabis is getting in the way at home or work, talk with a health care provider, or call SAMHSA's free National Helpline.

SAMHSA's National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357. It's free.

Our budtenders can talk about products, formats, aroma, and what's on the label, but we're not doctors, and we don't diagnose or recommend cannabis for any condition. Cannabis can change how some medications work, so if you have a health condition or take medication, talk with your health care provider or pharmacist first. See our medical cannabis page for where Minnesota's program lives.
