Around Ridgefield

Before or After Your Delivery Order

Defined by its rolling hills, distinct neighborhoods, and a location that puts Manhattan just 15 miles away, Ridgefield offers more than its modest footprint might suggest. From preserved wetlands and woodland trails to an eclectic dining scene and neighborhood bars with real character, there's always a reason to get out and explore. Place your order for the weed delivery Ridgefield locals love, and let the borough's hills, flavor, and character do the rest.

Veterans Memorial Park

A well-maintained community anchor on Shaler Boulevard, Veterans Memorial Park offers sports fields and courts, walking paths, and shaded picnic areas with covered tables in a clean, welcoming setting. Drawing residents from across the borough's three neighborhoods year-round, it serves as a place for both quiet remembrance and active events.

Visit02

Skeetkill Creek Marsh

A 16-acre preserved wetland along Bellman's Creek, Skeetkill Creek Marsh was enhanced in 1998 through a collaboration with Ducks Unlimited to restore open water habitat and create scrub-shrub environments that now support a diverse range of waterfowl and wildlife. A small public park with benches and an informational kiosk makes it an accessible and quietly rewarding place to spend time outdoors.

Visit03

Ridgefield Nature Center

Part of the Meadowlands trail system, the Ridgefield Nature Center is a 5.4-acre woodland preserve built around Trinity Spring, the historic natural source behind the Great Bear water bottling plant. The site features a hiking path that climbs to a hilltop overlook, a spring-fed pool that draws notable local wildlife, and a sense of quiet that's rare this close to the New York metro area.

Visit04

Cafe Tivoli

The Stancato family has been welcoming guests to Cafe Tivoli on Shaler Boulevard for years, building a reputation as one of Bergen County's standout Italian restaurants. Fresh, homemade dishes made with quality ingredients, private party rooms for special occasions, and a warm, unpretentious atmosphere make it a go-to for both date nights and larger gatherings.

Visit05

ARIA Korean Cuisine

Named after the Italian opera term for a distinctive solo performance, ARIA on Broad Avenue brings authentic Korean cuisine with a modern sensibility to Ridgefield's dining scene. The menu spans SSAM, cold buckwheat noodles, seafood pancakes, Korean beef tartare, and elevated side dishes that arrive looking as impressive as they taste.

The Colonial Bar

A neighborhood fixture on Grand Avenue, The Colonial Bar is the kind of unpretentious local spot that earns its loyal following with good drinks, a pool table, darts, happy-hour specials, and a genuinely welcoming atmosphere. It's a place where the regulars know each other and first-timers quickly feel at home.

What age requirement applies to cannabis delivery in Ridgefield?

New Jersey sets the minimum age for adult-use cannabis at 21, and that applies to every delivery order without exception. Your age is confirmed during account setup, and your driver will ask to see your ID again at the door before completing the handoff.

What forms of ID are accepted for delivery?

All you need is a valid, government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license, state-issued ID, or passport; out-of-state IDs are welcome. Upload your ID to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and have the physical copy ready when your driver arrives.

Is a medical cannabis card required to receive delivery in Ridgefield?

No, a medical card isn’t required at Lady L. We’re an adult-use dispensary, meaning the door is open to any customer 21 or older. Your Lady L account and a valid ID are the only credentials required for delivery.

How much cannabis can a single Ridgefield delivery order include?

Per New Jersey law, each transaction is capped at the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis. That amount can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What are the taxes on a Ridgefield cannabis delivery order?

Every adult-use cannabis purchase in New Jersey carries a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). Ridgefield weed delivery orders are taxed the same way as in-store purchases, and the full breakdown appears at checkout before you confirm.
