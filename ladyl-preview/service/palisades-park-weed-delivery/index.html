How Cannabis Delivery in Palisades Park Works

Getting a Palisades Park weed delivery order placed with Lady L is quick and straightforward. The whole process takes just minutes, and every step is clear before your order heads out the door.

What age do you have to be to receive cannabis delivery?

You must be 21 or older to place a delivery order with Lady L. New Jersey adult-use cannabis regulations apply to every transaction. Age is verified when you set up your account, and your driver will check your ID at the door before completing the handoff.

What kind of ID is required for Lady L cannabis delivery?

Any valid government-issued photo ID is accepted, including driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and you'll need the physical ID on hand when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical cannabis card to place a delivery order?

Not at all. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary, which means any customer 21 or older can place a delivery order without a medical card. A valid ID and a confirmed Lady L account are all that's required to get started.

What’s the purchase limit on a single Palisades Park weed delivery?

New Jersey law permits us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. Here's how that breaks down by product type:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis delivery orders in Palisades Park?

Adult-use cannabis in New Jersey is subject to a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These apply to delivery orders the same way they apply to in-store purchases. All charges are broken down at checkout before you confirm your order.

How does payment work for delivery orders?

We accept ACH and debit payments for delivery orders, but not cash. You’ll set up your preferred payment method through your Lady L account when you register. For specific payment questions, contact us at the store directly at 201-595-0706.

What if my delivery is running late?

Typical delivery time is approximately one hour, though traffic, order volume, and distance can all affect timing on any given run. If your order is taking longer than expected, get in touch with our team. We stay communicative when delays come up and work to keep every delivery on track.

Can I change or cancel my order after it's been submitted?

Reach out as quickly as possible if something needs to change. Once a driver is dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Contact us through the website or by phone at 201-595-0706.

Does someone need to come to the door when my delivery arrives?

Yes. An adult 21 or older must be available and ready to present a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is there to accept the delivery, the order cannot be completed. Make sure you're reachable during your estimated delivery window.

Does Lady L offer deals on Palisades Park weed delivery orders?

Always! Our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, so it's always worth checking with our team or browsing the promotions section on the website for what's currently running.

Meanwhile, loyalty points apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases, so you're always earning. Every dollar spent earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit. Lady L also offers dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip my delivery driver?

Tipping is always appreciated but never required. Our drivers handle every delivery with professionalism and discretion, and a tip in the range of 15-20% is a genuine way to recognize that effort.
