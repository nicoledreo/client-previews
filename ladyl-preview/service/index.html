Pro tips

Delivery Tips for the Best Experience

  • Double-check your address before placing your order.
  • Make sure your government-issued ID is current.
  • Keep your phone nearby after placing your order.
  • Answer calls or text messages from our delivery team if they need assistance locating your address
  • Plan to receive your delivery at the address provided during checkout.
  • Order early during weekends and holidays for the greatest scheduling flexibility.

Important Reminder

New Jersey law only allows cannabis deliveries to private residences and approved delivery locations. Deliveries to public places—including parks, beaches, bars, restaurants, parking lots, or other public spaces—are prohibited.

Explore Our Curated New Jersey Weed Delivery Menu

Lady L doesn't try to carry everything. We carry the right things. Every product on our menu is selected for quality, consistency, and value. Our team knows the brands, the growers, and the products we stock, and that knowledge shows in every recommendation we make.

Flower is our top-selling category and for good reason. We stock fresh, high-quality cannabis from trusted brands like Grown Rogue, Happy Farmer, Hamilton Farms, Rythm, Yeti, Good Greens, and Tyson. Our flower selection covers a range of effects and price points to suit every customer, whether you're brand new or know exactly what you want.

Pre-rolls offer a convenient, ready-to-go option for customers who want quality without preparation. Our vaporizer selection brings clean, discreet cannabis experiences in a format that travels well and hits consistently.

Edibles are a favorite for customers who prefer a longer-lasting, non-smokable experience. Concentrates round out our menu for those who want potency and precision.

We also carry topicals and tinctures for customers exploring cannabis wellness in more targeted ways. All products comply with New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory Commission guidelines. Pricing across our menu ranges from $10 to $150, making quality cannabis genuinely accessible at every budget.

Per New Jersey regulations, each transaction allows up to 28.35 grams of flower, up to 4 grams of concentrates, or up to 1,000 mg of THC in edibles. You can mix and match product types within a single order as long as you stay within the equivalent of one ounce of usable cannabis.
