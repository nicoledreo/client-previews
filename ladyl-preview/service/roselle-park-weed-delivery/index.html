How old do you need to be to get cannabis delivery in Roselle Park?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law is straightforward on this: you must be 21 or older to place a delivery order. Age is verified when you create your Lady L account, and your driver will confirm your ID at the door before the handoff is completed. No exceptions apply.

Which IDs does Lady L accept for delivery orders?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID is accepted. That includes driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and the physical ID must be present when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical card to get Roselle Park weed delivery?

No medical card is required. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are all you need to place your first order.

How much cannabis can I include in a single delivery order?

New Jersey law allows up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That total can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to a Roselle Park cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a $ 2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All of these apply equally to delivery orders, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.

How can I pay for a Lady L delivery order?

Delivery orders are processed through your Lady L account using ACH payment. A debit option is also available. Cash is not accepted for delivery. Full payment must be submitted before your order is confirmed and dispatched. For any payment questions, reach out to us directly at 201-595-0706.

If a Roselle Park weed delivery runs late, what happens?

Typical delivery time runs approximately one hour, though order volume, traffic, and distance can all be factors. We keep a close eye on every order and flag delays as soon as we're aware of them. If your order is running late, you can always reach out to our team directly.

Can I change or cancel my order once it's been placed?

Get in touch with us as soon as possible if something needs to change. Once an order is dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us by phone at 201-595-0706 or through the website.

Will someone need to be here when my delivery arrives?

Yes. An adult 21 or older must be available and ready to present a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is home at the time of delivery, the order cannot be completed. Stay available during your estimated delivery window to keep things on track.

Are there any deals or discounts available on delivery orders?

Absolutely. While delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, loyalty points apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases. Every dollar spent earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit.

Discount programs are available for first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several other groups. Deals rotate, so reach out to the team or check the promotions section for what's currently active.

Should I tip my Roselle Park delivery driver?

Tipping is never required, but it's always a welcome gesture. Our drivers bring genuine care and professionalism to every order, and for reliable weed delivery Roselle Park residents appreciate, a tip of 15 to 20% is a great way to recognize that.
