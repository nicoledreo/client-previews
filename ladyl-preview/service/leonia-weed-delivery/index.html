Around Leonia

Before or After Your Delivery Order

Leonia earned its nickname, the Athens of New Jersey, honestly. The borough has been drawing artists, intellectuals, and community-minded residents to its tree-lined streets since the late 1800s, and that creative energy hasn't faded. Broad Avenue runs through the heart of the commercial district; Hillside Avenue adds its own quieter character; and the residential blocks between Fort Lee Road and Christie Heights Street give the borough the kind of settled, walkable feel that's hard to manufacture. Weed delivery Leonia residents rely on from Lady L means your plans stay on your terms, whether you'll be heading out to spend time in town or relaxing at home for the rest of the day.

01

Erika and David Boyd Sculpture Garden

The Erika and David Boyd Sculpture Garden at Broad Avenue and Beechwood Place is an outdoor exhibition space maintained by Sculpture for Leonia, an all-volunteer nonprofit founded in 2008. Works by artists from around the world are displayed on rotating loans, with some pieces donated permanently to the borough. Additional sculptures installed through the program can be found at the public library and in local parks. The organization's annual Taste of Leonia fundraiser, held each fall in the garden, brings together food, wine, live music, and a silent auction in one of the borough's most distinctive public spaces.

Visit02

Civil War Drill Hall Theatre

Built in 1859 at 130 Grand Avenue, the Civil War Drill Hall is home to the Players Guild of Leonia, founded in 1919 and recognized as New Jersey's oldest continuing community theater group. The guild grew out of the same creative spirit that made Leonia a destination for artists across three generations, and it has maintained an unbroken run of community productions ever since. Five minutes from the George Washington Bridge, the theater draws audiences from well beyond the borough for its annual season of plays and musicals performed entirely by local talent.

Visit03

Wood Park

Wood Park at 370 Broad Avenue is Leonia's most fully equipped public park, with three tennis courts, a tennis wall, basketball courts, a softball field, multiple picnic areas, and a shelter house with restrooms. The park shares a parking lot with the Leonia Public Library on Fort Lee Road, making it a natural anchor for the borough's civic and recreational life. Mature trees and open lawns give it a quiet, unhurried character that draws residents year-round.

Visit04

Leonia Public Library

The Leonia Public Library is one of the more fully featured public libraries in the area for its size. Beyond a strong circulating collection, it offers a Library of Things that includes board games, mobile hotspots, classic video games, and electronics kits, along with 3D printing access, ESL classes, tech assistance appointments, museum passes, a seed library, and a digital collection spanning Libby, Hoopla, Kanopy, and Mango language learning. The library also hosts rotating monthly art exhibitions, a nod to the borough's deep creative roots.

Visit05

Choo Choo Train Sushi

Choo Choo Train at 374 Broad Avenue is a conveyor belt sushi restaurant built around a train-themed concept that's as much about the experience as the food. A miniature train delivers dishes directly to tables, while a full menu of specialty rolls and à la carte selections ensures there's something for every preference. The kitchen consistently produces highly rated sushi with creative rolls, fresh ingredients, and vegan options alongside traditional offerings, earning the restaurant a loyal following along Leonia's main commercial corridor.

Visit06

Rumba Cafe

Rumba Cafe on Hillside Avenue serves authentic Colombian and Cuban cuisine in a warm, no-frills setting that regulars return for specifically because of the quality of the cooking. The menu centers on traditional recipes made with fresh ingredients, with standout dishes including ajiaco, a rich traditional Colombian soup; bandeja paisa; coconut rice; Cuban sandwiches; and freshly made tostones. It's the kind of neighborhood spot that builds its reputation one honest plate at a time.

How old do you have to be to receive cannabis delivery in Leonia?

Per New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law, the minimum age is 21, and every Lady L delivery order adheres to that without exception. Age is verified when you register your account, and your driver will confirm your ID upon arrival before the handoff.

What counts as valid ID for a Lady L cannabis delivery?

Government-issued photo ID is the standard. Driver's licenses, state-issued identification cards, and passports all work, and out-of-state IDs are accepted. You'll need to upload your ID to your Lady L account before your first order and have the physical document on hand when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical cannabis card to use Lady L's delivery service?

No. Lady L operates entirely under adult-use rules, which means the process is open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed account and a valid ID are the only requirements. Medical status is never a factor.

What's the most cannabis I can order at one time in Leonia?

New Jersey caps each transaction at one ounce, or 28.35 grams, of usable cannabis. That ceiling covers all product types combined, broken down as follows:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
Are there taxes on cannabis delivery orders in Leonia?

Yes, and they're straightforward. New Jersey applies a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax in municipalities that have authorized it. Every charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm, so you know exactly what you're paying before you commit.

What are my payment options for a Lady L delivery?

Lady L accepts ACH payment and a debit option for delivery customers, but cash is off the table. Set up your payment method in your Lady L account when you register. We collect payment in full before your order is dispatched. Questions? Call us at 201-595-0706.

My Leonia delivery hasn't arrived yet. What should I do?

Most orders arrive within an hour, though timing depends on order volume, your position in the queue, how far our drivers are traveling, and traffic conditions. If you're past the one-hour mark, reach out directly. We track every order and won't leave you guessing.

I need to change something about my order. Is that possible?

Maybe, but speed matters. Get in touch the moment you realize something needs to change. Orders that have already been dispatched are much harder to modify, and in some cases, it may not be possible. Call 201-595-0706 or use the website to reach us as quickly as you can, and we’ll do our best to help.

Do I have to come to the door when my delivery arrives?

An adult 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID must be present and ready when the driver arrives. That's a firm requirement on every delivery, without exception. If no one who meets that requirement is available, the handoff can’t be completed.

What delivery deals are available for Leonia customers?

Lady L’s rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, so checking with our team or keeping an eye on the promotions section of the website are the best ways to stay current on what's available for delivery customers.

Meanwhile, loyalty points stack up on delivery orders at the same rate as in-store purchases, turning every dollar spent into one point toward store credit. One hundred points converts to $5. Lady L also runs dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip my Lady L delivery driver?

Tipping is never expected, but it's always a reflection of the experience. When a driver shows up on time, handles the exchange professionally, and makes the whole thing feel effortless, a tip in the 15-20% range is a solid way to say, “Well done!"
