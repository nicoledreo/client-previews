How Cannabis Delivery in Elizabeth Works

The process is easy to follow from start to finish. Here's exactly what to expect when you place your first Elizabeth weed delivery with Lady L.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Set Up Your Lady L Account

    Before placing your first delivery order, you'll need to create an account through our online ordering platform by uploading your current government-issued photo ID, verifying your delivery address, and adding your mobile phone number. Having accurate information on file allows our team to process your order quickly and contact you if we need assistance locating your address.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Browse the Menu and Add to Cart

    Head to the delivery menu and browse what's available. The menu is updated regularly, so what you see reflects current stock. Add your favorites to the cart and review your order before checkout.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Submit Payment

    Once you've selected your products, choose your preferred payment method — either Pay by Bank or Pay by Text. To avoid any delays, please ensure payment is completed before checking out, as only fully paid orders are eligible for delivery dispatch.

    Step 4: Expect Your Driver in About an Hour

    Once your order is confirmed, a driver will head your way. Typical delivery time is about one hour. Your driver will contact you upon arrival and verify your valid government-issued ID before completing the handoff. That's it.

Delivery FAQ

Elizabeth Weed Delivery FAQ

Where does Lady L offer weed delivery in Elizabeth, NJ?

Lady L delivers across four Elizabeth ZIP codes: 07201, 07202, 07206, and 07208. That covers the full city from Elmora and the North End down through Elizabethport and the neighborhoods along the waterfront.

What are the Elizabeth weed delivery hours?

Elizabeth cannabis delivery runs Sunday from noon to 7:00 PM, Monday through Thursday from noon to 8:00 PM, and Friday and Saturday from noon to 9:00 PM. Orders need to be placed and confirmed before closing time. Weekend hours give you a wider window for those later evenings when you need something delivered.

What's the minimum age for Elizabeth weed delivery?

Adult-use cannabis delivery in New Jersey requires you to be 21 or older. That applies to placing the order, holding the account, and accepting the delivery at the door. There are no exceptions, and your driver will always verify your ID upon arrival.

What ID do I need for cannabis delivery in Elizabeth?

A valid, government-issued photo ID is required. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify, including IDs issued outside of New Jersey. Upload your ID to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and keep the physical ID on hand when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical card for delivery?

No medical card is needed. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary, and our delivery service works the same way. If you're 21 or older with a valid ID and an active account, you're good to order. No referral, no prescription, no extra steps.

How much cannabis can I buy in one delivery order?

We are allowed to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. This can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, such as purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis delivery in Elizabeth?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These apply to delivery orders the same as in-store purchases. All applicable taxes are shown at checkout before you confirm your order.

What payment methods are accepted for delivery?

All Elizabeth weed delivery orders require full payment to be submitted and captured before the order is placed and dispatched. If you have questions on how to pay for your delivery, please contact the store directly at 201-595-0706. Set up your payment through your Lady L account when you create your profile. Cash is not accepted for delivery orders.

What if my delivery takes longer than expected?

Most Elizabeth deliveries arrive within about an hour, but traffic, order volume, and distance can all affect timing. If things run long, our team will reach out proactively. For any questions while you wait, call (201) 595-0706, and we'll give you a real update.

Can I cancel or modify my delivery order?

Contact us as quickly as possible if you need to make a change. Once an order is dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us through the website or by phone at (201) 595-0706.

Does someone have to be home to accept the delivery?

Yes. A person 21 or older must be at the address and ready to show a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If nobody's available at delivery time, the order can't be completed. Build your delivery window around a time you know you'll be home.

Are there deals on weed delivery orders?

Delivery orders earn loyalty points at the same rate as in-store purchases, so every dollar spent counts toward your next reward. Every 100 points earns $5 in store credit. Discounts for first-time customers, veterans, seniors, and other groups may apply, though availability varies. Check the current promotions on the website or ask our team what's active when you order.

Should I tip my Lady L delivery driver?

Tips aren't required, but our drivers genuinely appreciate them. They work hard to make every Elizabeth delivery smooth, fast, and discreet. If your driver showed up on time and handled things well, a tip of 15–20% is a great way to show it.
