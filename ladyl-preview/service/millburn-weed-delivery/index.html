Millburn Weed Delivery FAQ

Does Lady L Cannabis deliver to all of Millburn Township, NJ?

Yes. Lady L delivers to both Millburn ZIP codes, 07041 for the primary downtown area and 07078 for Short Hills, as part of our Zone 2 service area. Our drivers head out from West Side Avenue and reach customers across a wide stretch of northern New Jersey.

Beyond Millburn, our delivery footprint extends across Bergen, Hudson, Essex, Union, and Passaic counties. From neighboring towns like Maplewood and Summit to communities further out across the region, the coverage area is broad and growing.

What’s the minimum age for cannabis delivery in Millburn?

You must be 21 or older to place a delivery order with Lady L. New Jersey's adult-use cannabis laws apply to every transaction. Age is verified during account setup, and your driver will confirm your ID at the door before completing the handoff.

What kind of ID do I need for a Lady L delivery?

Any valid government-issued photo ID will do, including driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before you can place your first order, and you'll need the physical ID ready when your driver arrives.

Is a medical cannabis card required to receive delivery in Millburn?

No medical card is necessary. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, so any customer 21 or older can place a delivery order. All you need is a valid ID and a confirmed Lady L account.

How much cannabis can I order in a single Millburn delivery?

New Jersey law allows us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. Here's how that breaks down:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes will I pay on a Millburn cannabis delivery order?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey carry a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These apply to delivery orders just as they do to in-store purchases. All applicable charges are itemized at checkout before you confirm.

How does payment work for Lady L delivery orders?

We accept ACH and debit payments for delivery orders; however, cash is not an option. Set up your preferred payment method through your Lady L account when you register. For specific payment questions, reach the store directly at 201-595-0706.

What if my delivery takes longer than expected?

While the typical delivery time is approximately one hour, order volume, distance, and traffic can all cause things to take a bit longer. If your order is running behind schedule, reach out to our team. We stay on top of delays and communicate proactively to keep every delivery moving as smoothly as possible.

Can I make any changes to my order after it's placed?

Contact us as quickly as possible if something needs to be adjusted. Once an order is dispatched, changes may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us through the website or by phone at 201-595-0706.

Does someone need to be home when my delivery arrives?

Yes. An adult 21 or older must be present and ready to show a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the driver can’t complete the handoff, so please make sure you're available during your estimated delivery window.

Does Lady L offer deals or discounts on Millburn delivery orders?

We do! Our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, though, so it's always worth checking with our team or browsing the promotions section on the website to see what's currently active.

Meanwhile, loyalty points apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases. That means that every dollar spent on Millburn weed delivery earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit. Lady L also offers dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip my Lady L delivery driver?

Tipping is optional, but our drivers genuinely earn it. Every delivery is handled with professionalism, discretion, and care, and a tip in the range of 15-20% is a meaningful way to show appreciation for a job well done.
