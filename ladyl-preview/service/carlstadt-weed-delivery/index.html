Around Carlstadt

Before or After Your Delivery Order

Carlstadt makes the most of its location. The borough sits right next to the Meadowlands sports and entertainment complex, putting residents within easy reach of MetLife Stadium and major concert venues, while the local dining and craft beer scene gives the community its own distinct identity right at home. Getting the weed delivery Carlstadt customers can count on from Lady L means your plans stay in place, whether you're heading out to one of the borough's standout spots or settling in at home.

Bolera Snort Brewery

At 316 20th Street, Bolero Snort has been crafting hoof-inspired ales and lagers in Carlstadt since 2013, opening its own production facility and tasting room in 2020 after years of contract brewing across the tristate area. The taproom pours a rotating selection of classic and experimental styles, from crisp lagers to bold IPAs and creative sour ales, with a beer garden, food truck partnerships, and private event space rounding out the experience. Its location just a short walk from MetLife Stadium, has made it a natural gathering spot for pre- and post-event crowds.

Visit02

Carlstadt Historical Society Firehouse Museum

Housed in the borough's 1919 firehouse at Division and Sixth, the Carlstadt Historical Society museum preserves the community's story across two floors of exhibits. The ground floor covers WWI and WWII memorabilia from local residents, police and fire department artifacts, and a collection of bottles manufactured by Carlstadt's once-thriving glass industry. The upper floor is dedicated to the borough's school history, with class photos, graduation pennants from what is recognized as the oldest continuous school picnic in the United States, and donated items from former businesses that once lined the town's streets.

Visit03

River Barge Park

River Barge Park is a 5.5-acre waterfront park on the Hackensack River managed by the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority. The park offers a boat ramp, paddling and fishing dock, a scenic promenade lined with native plantings, picnic tables, and catch-and-release fishing. The site has a layered history, having served as a WWII seaplane training base in the 1940s before becoming a popular local restaurant, known as the Barge Club, in the ‘70s and ‘80s. Nautical artifacts from that era are displayed throughout the park today.

Visit04

Redd's Restaurant & Biergarten

Redd's at 317 Washington Avenue is a casual bar and restaurant with an outdoor biergarten built around long communal tables, an extensive global beer selection, and 35-plus flat screens for sports viewing. The venue serves as a natural hub on event days at nearby MetLife Stadium, offering shuttle service to and from the complex for major events. A second-level banquet facility accommodates private gatherings from 30 to 200 guests, making it one of the more versatile event spaces in the area.

La Fortaleza brings authentic Mexican cuisine to Carlstadt as part of a multi-location operation spanning several communities across New Jersey and New York. The menu centers on traditional recipes and fresh ingredients, with tacos, enchiladas, burritos, and margaritas anchoring a lineup that has earned La Fortaleza a loyal following across the region. The Carlstadt location brings that same commitment to flavor and authenticity to the heart of the borough.

Il Villaggio at 651 Route 17 North has been serving traditional Italian cuisine in Carlstadt for nearly four decades, earning a reputation as one of the most authentic Italian dining experiences in the region. Professional chefs work exclusively with fresh ingredients, rotating daily specials alongside a full menu of classic dishes prepared with consistency and care. An intimate yet spacious dining room and a dedicated banquet operation make it equally suited to a quiet dinner and a large private celebration.

How old do you have to be to place a cannabis delivery order?

Per New Jersey's adult-use cannabis laws, the minimum age is 21, and that applies to every order Lady L processes without exception. Age is verified during account registration, and your driver will confirm your ID upon arrival before completing the handoff.

What ID does Lady L require for cannabis delivery?

A valid government-issued photo ID is required twice, first uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and then presented in person when your driver arrives. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify. Unexpired out-of-state IDs are accepted without issue.

Is a medical cannabis card needed to receive Lady L's Carlstadt weed delivery?

No medical card is required. Lady L is a fully adult-use operation, open to any customer 21 or older with a valid ID and a confirmed account. No referral, no card, and no special status needed.

What are the per-order purchase limits for cannabis delivery in Carlstadt?

New Jersey sets the per-transaction limit at the equivalent of one ounce, or 28.35 grams, of usable cannabis. Here's how that breaks down by product type:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What will the taxes be on my Carlstadt cannabis delivery order?

Three charges apply to adult-use cannabis in New Jersey: a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where the local municipality has authorized it. Every charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm, so there are no surprises.

How does payment work for weed delivery Carlstadt residents order from Lady L?

We collect payment in full before your order is placed and we send a driver your way. Lady L accepts ACH payment and a debit option for delivery customers. Cash is not accepted for delivery orders. Set up your payment method through your Lady L account at registration, and call 201-595-0706 with any specific payment questions.

What should I do if my delivery is running behind schedule?

One hour is the typical window, but order volume, distance, and road conditions can all affect timing on any given day. If your order is taking longer than expected, reach out to our team directly, and we'll give you a straight answer on where things stand.

Can I make changes to my order or cancel it once it's been submitted?

The sooner you contact us, the better. Once a driver is dispatched, making changes to an order may not be possible. Call us at 201-595-0706 or reach out through the website, and our team will do whatever we can to help.

Does Lady L offer Carlstadt weed delivery deals or discounts?

Yep! Lady L does offer deals, although our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions. Checking with our team or keeping an eye on the promotions section of the website is the best way to stay up to date on what's available to delivery customers.

Every delivery order also earns loyalty points at the same rate as in-store purchases. A dollar spent is a point earned, and once you hit 100 points, that converts to $5 in store credit that can be applied to future orders. On top of that, Lady L runs dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Is tipping the delivery drivers expected?

Tipping is always optional, but our drivers consistently bring care, discretion, and professionalism to every delivery. If your experience hit the mark from start to finish, a tip in the 15-20% range is a meaningful way to acknowledge that.
