How it works

How Cannabis Delivery in Roselle Works

Placing a Roselle weed delivery order with Lady L is a short, well-defined process that gives you a professional, friendly driver and a clear timeline. Here's how it unfolds.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Set Up Your Lady L Account

    Before placing your first delivery order, you'll need to create an account through our online ordering platform by uploading your current government-issued photo ID, verifying your delivery address, and adding your mobile phone number. Having accurate information on file allows our team to process your order quickly and contact you if we need assistance locating your address.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Browse the Menu and Add to Cart

    Head to the delivery menu and take your time to browse the different formats. Stock is updated regularly, so every item you see is available. Make your selections, review the cart, and proceed to checkout when you're satisfied.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Submit Payment

    Once you've selected your products, choose your preferred payment method — either Pay by Bank or Pay by Text. To avoid any delays, please ensure payment is completed before checking out, as only fully paid orders are eligible for delivery dispatch.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Expect Your Driver in About an Hour

    Once your order is confirmed, a driver is en route. Most deliveries arrive in approximately one hour. Your driver will contact you and verify your valid government-issued ID before the handoff is complete.

What age do you need to be for cannabis delivery in Roselle?

Per New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law, the minimum age is 21, and every Lady L delivery order adheres to that rule without exception. Your age is verified when you first register your Lady L account, and your driver will confirm your ID upon arrival before the handoff.

What ID does Lady L accept?

Any valid government-issued photo ID will work, including driver's licenses, state-issued identification cards, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are accepted. You’ll upload your ID to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and keep the physical document on hand when your driver arrives.

Is a medical cannabis card required to place a Roselle delivery order?

No medical card is necessary. Lady L operates as a fully adult-use cannabis microdispensary, which means the process is open to any customer 21 or older with a valid ID and a confirmed account. Nothing else is required.

What are the purchase limits for cannabis delivery orders?

New Jersey caps each transaction at one ounce, or 28.35 grams, of usable cannabis across all product types combined. Here's how that breaks down:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to Roselle weed delivery orders?

There are three taxes that apply to New Jersey adult-use cannabis: a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-perounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax in municipalities that have authorized it. All applicable charges appear at checkout, so there’s full transparency about what you’re paying for.

How does payment work for Lady L delivery orders in Roselle?

Before a driver is dispatched, full payment needs to be submitted. Lady L offers ACH payment and a debit option, but cash is not accepted for delivery orders. The easiest way to handle it is to set up your preferred payment method through your Lady L account when you register, so everything is ready to go when you place your order. Questions about the payment process? Call 201-595-0706.

What happens if a Lady L delivery runs late?

One hour is the typical window, but order volume, traffic, and distance can all shift that on any given run. If your delivery is taking longer than expected, reach out to our team directly. We track every order and won't leave you without a straight answer on where things stand.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Possibly, but the sooner you contact us, the better. Once a driver is en route, modifications become difficult and may be impossible to accommodate. Call 201-595-0706 or reach us through the website as quickly as you can, and our team will do everything we can to help.

Who needs to be present when my delivery arrives?

Someone 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID has to be at the delivery address when the driver shows up. That's a firm requirement every time. If no qualifying adult is available at the time of the handoff, the order can’t be completed, so make sure you're reachable during your estimated window.

Does Lady L offer deals on Roselle weed delivery orders?

Lady L does offer delivery deals, and our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions. So, checking with our team and keeping an eye on the promotions section of the website are the best ways to stay current on what's available for delivery customers.

Also, every delivery order earns loyalty points at the same rate as in-store purchases, which means every dollar you spend equals 1 point, and one hundred points convert to $5 in store credit. On top of that, Lady L runs dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip my delivery driver?

Tipping is optional and never expected, but it's always a reflection of the experience. When a driver shows up on time, handles everything professionally, and makes the whole exchange feel effortless on your end, a tip in the 15-20% range is a meaningful way to say so.
