Around Little Ferry

Before or After Your Delivery Order

Little Ferry, NJ, is a place that doesn't announce itself loudly but has plenty going on. The dining scene along Main Street and Sylvan Avenue reflects a genuinely diverse community; the natural spaces along the Hackensack River offer a quieter side of life; and Washington Avenue carries a history that stretches back to the Revolutionary War. Getting weed delivery Little Ferry residents rely on from Lady L means your order is ready when you are, whether you're home along Bergen Turnpike, on Colonial Court, or in the residential blocks off Liberty Street.

Palermo’s Cafe & Bakery

Located at 399 Liberty Street, Palermo's has been a Little Ferry institution since 1989, serving artisan brick-oven pizzas, paninis, sandwiches, and a full lineup of traditional Italian gourmet desserts that have earned it a devoted following well beyond the borough. The cafe also offers beer, wine, and cocktails alongside its food menu, and the custom cake shop has become one of the more sought-after destinations in the area for celebration cakes and specialty desserts. Indoor seating and a patio make it a natural gathering spot in any season.

Nikki’s Bar & Grill

Nikki's on Washington Avenue has established itself as one of Little Ferry's most lively spots since opening in 2017. The bar and grill features 14 TVs for sports viewing, eight draft beers, more than 25 bottled selections, and a menu of American and BBQ dishes headlined by the Tres Golpes burger and a rotating lineup of tacos and wings. Karaoke on Tuesdays and live DJs or bands on weekends keep the atmosphere consistently energetic, with a late-night kitchen running well past midnight most nights.

Visit03

Losen Slote Creek Park

Losen Slote Creek Park occupies 28 acres of lowland forest and meadow right in Little Ferry, preserved since 1990 through a cooperative agreement with the New Jersey Meadowlands Commission. What makes the park distinctive is its layered natural history: the Losen Slote Creek itself was once a tidal tributary of the Hackensack River, later impounded to create the wetland habitat that now borders Mehrhof Pond, a former clay mining site that has gradually transformed into one of the area's most productive wildlife habitats. The trail through the park passes through a rich mix of damp woodland, shrub thicket, and open meadow, with botanical diversity and birdlife that reward visitors in every season.

Visit04

Whiskey Roads Sports Bar

Whiskey Roads at 152 Washington Avenue describes itself as a premium dive experience, where the energy of a neighborhood tavern meets the atmosphere of a high-end whiskey lounge. The family-owned bar hosts live music, DJs, and comedy shows in partnership with the Meadowlands Comedy Club, along with a food menu that includes lunch specials, half-baby-back ribs, porterhouse steak, and a late-night kitchen that runs until 2:30 AM on weekends. The polished concrete floors and signature neon lighting give it a character that's distinctly its own.

Visit05

Victoria's Mexican-American Grill

Victoria's at 201 Main Street is a mom-and-pop Mexican-American restaurant that has quietly built one of the strongest reputations on the local dining scene. The menu spans tacos, fajitas, chimichangas, coconut shrimp, housemade guacamole, and churros, with complimentary chips and black bean dip arriving at the table as a matter of course. Ranked among the top restaurants in the borough, it earns its following one honest, flavorful plate at a time. BYOB.

Visit06

Lakeview Field

Lakeview Field, at 20 Indian Lake Road, sits along the shores of Indian Lake and is one of Little Ferry's most active outdoor recreational spaces. Baseball fields, open lawns, and access to waterside fishing make it a natural draw for residents year-round. The lakeside setting gives it a calm, unhurried character that feels removed from the borough's busier commercial corridors, yet is still close to everything.

Little Ferry Weed Delivery FAQ

What age do you need to be to receive a Lady L delivery in Little Ferry?

Per New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law, the minimum age is 21, and every Lady L delivery order adheres to that rule without exception. Your age is verified when you first register your Lady L account, and your driver will confirm your ID upon arrival before the handoff.

What identification does Lady L accept for cannabis delivery?

Government-issued photo ID is what you need, and the most common forms all qualify: driver's licenses, state-issued identification cards, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are accepted. Have your ID uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and keep the physical document on hand when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical cannabis card to place a Little Ferry delivery order?

No medical card is necessary. Lady L operates as a fully adult-use cannabis microdispensary, which means the process is open to any customer 21 or older with a valid ID and a confirmed account. Nothing else is required.

What's the purchase limit for a single delivery order?

New Jersey caps each transaction at one ounce, or 28.35 grams, of usable cannabis. That ceiling covers all product types combined, broken down as follows:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes come with a Little Ferry cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis carries three charges: a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-perounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax in municipalities that have authorized it. All applicable charges appear at checkout before your order is finalized, so there are no surprises on the back end.

How does Lady L handle payment for delivery?

Payment goes through in full before your order is confirmed and before we dispatch your delivery driver. Lady L accepts ACH payment and a debit option for delivery customers, but not cash. You’ll set up your payment method in your Lady L account at the time of registration. For specific questions, call us at 201-595-0706.

What should I do if my delivery is running late?

Reach out directly if your order is taking longer than the typical one-hour window. Distance, traffic, and order volume can all shift timing on any given run, and our team stays on top of every order in the queue. We won't leave you guessing about where things stand.

I need to change or cancel my order. Is that possible?

Speed matters here. Get in touch the moment something needs to change, because once a driver is dispatched, modifications may not be possible. Call 201-595-0706 or reach us through the website, and our team will do everything we can to accommodate you.

Does someone need to be present when my delivery arrives?

Yes, and that requirement is firm. An adult 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID must be there when the driver arrives. Packages can’t be left unattended, so if no one who meets the age and ID requirements is available at the time, the handoff can’t be completed. Make sure you're reachable during your estimated window.

What deals or discounts are available for Little Ferry weed delivery customers?

Lady L’s rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, so checking with our team or keeping tabs on the promotions section of the website are the best ways to stay up to date on what's available to delivery customers.

Meanwhile, loyalty points stack up on delivery orders at the same rate as in-store purchases: every dollar spent earns one point, and 100 points convert to $5. Lady L also runs dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip my delivery driver?

Our drivers never expect a tip, but they consistently earn one. Every delivery is handled with care, discretion, and a professionalism that makes the whole experience feel easy. If yours did, a tip in the 15-20% range is a solid way to show you appreciate the driver's effort.
