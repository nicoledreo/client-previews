How old do you have to be to use Lady L's Wood-Ridge cannabis delivery?

You must be 21 or older to place a delivery order. New Jersey's adult-use cannabis laws apply to every transaction, and age verification is built into the account setup process. Your driver will also confirm your ID at the door before completing the handoff.

What ID is accepted for cannabis delivery in Wood-Ridge?

Any valid government-issued photo ID works, including driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are accepted. You’ll need to upload your ID to your Lady L account before your first order and have the physical ID on hand when your driver arrives. That applies to every delivery and every customer.

Do I need a medical cannabis card to use Lady L's delivery service?

Not at all. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, which means any customer 21 or older can place a delivery order without a medical card. A valid ID and a confirmed Lady L account are all that's required.

How much cannabis can I receive in a single Wood-Ridge delivery order?

New Jersey law allows us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. Here's how that breaks down by product type:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply when you order cannabis delivery in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These taxes apply to delivery orders just as they do to in-store purchases. All applicable charges are shown at checkout before you confirm.

What happens if my Wood-Ridge delivery is running late?

Typical delivery time is approximately one hour, though volume, distance, and traffic can all affect timing. If your order is taking longer than expected, don’t hesitate to reach out to our team. We communicate proactively when delays come up and work to keep every delivery moving as efficiently as possible.

Can I modify or cancel my delivery order after it's placed?

Get in touch as quickly as possible if something needs to change. Once an order is dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us through the website or by phone at 201-595-0706.

Do I need to be home when my delivery arrives?

An adult 21 or older must be present and ready to show a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the order cannot be completed. Plan to be available during your estimated delivery window.

Does Lady L offer deals for Wood-Ridge delivery customers?

Absolutely. Our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, though, so it's always worth checking with our team or browsing the promotions section on the website for what's currently active.

Meanwhile, loyalty points apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases, so you're always earning. Every dollar spent earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit.Lady L also offers dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip my Lady L delivery driver?

Tipping is always optional, but it's genuinely appreciated. Our drivers take pride in professional, discreet service and go out of their way to make every delivery smooth. A tip in the range of 15-20% is a great way to recognize that effort.
