How old do I need to be to get cannabis delivery in Vauxhall?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law requires you to be 21 or older to place a delivery order. Age is verified when you set up your Lady L account, and your driver will check your ID at the door before the handoff is completed. No exceptions apply.

Which IDs does Lady L accept for delivery?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID works. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify, and out-of-state IDs are accepted, too. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and you’ll need to have the physical ID on hand when your driver arrives.

Do I also need a medical card to get cannabis delivered in Vauxhall?

No medical card is required. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are all you need to place your Vauxhall weed delivery order.

What's the purchase limit for a single order?

New Jersey law allows us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That can include any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis in New Jersey?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a $ 2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All of these apply equally to delivery orders, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.

How can I pay for a Lady L delivery order?

Delivery orders are processed through your Lady L account using ACH payment. A debit option is also available. Cash is not accepted for delivery. Full payment must be submitted before your order is confirmed and dispatched. For any payment questions, reach out to us directly at 201-595-0706.

What happens if my Vauxhall weed delivery runs late?

Typical delivery time is approximately one hour, but traffic, order volume, and distance can all affect timing. We track every order and communicate delays as proactively as we can. If your order is taking longer than expected, you can reach out to our team directly.

Is it possible to change or cancel my order after it’s been confirmed?

Contact us as soon as possible if anything needs to change. Once an order has been dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us by phone at 201-595-0706 or through the website.

Will I need to be at home when my delivery arrives?

An adult 21 or older must be present and ready to show a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the order cannot be completed. Stay available during your estimated delivery window to keep things on track.

What deals or discounts can I get on Vauxhall weed delivery orders?

Delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, but loyalty points apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases. Every dollar spent earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit.

We also have discount programs available for first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several other groups. Deals rotate regularly, so reach out to the team or check the promotions section for what's currently active.

How much should I tip my driver?

Tipping is never required, but it's always appreciated. For the reliable weed delivery Vauxhall residents count on, our drivers bring real professionalism and care to every order. A 15-20% tip is a great way to say thank you for a delivery job well done.
