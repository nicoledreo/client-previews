How Cannabis Delivery in Kenilworth Works

Ordering Kenilworth weed delivery through Lady L is a process designed to be clear and friction-free from the very first step. Here's what to expect.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Set Up Your Lady L Account

    Before placing your first delivery order, you'll need to create an account through our online ordering platform by uploading your current government-issued photo ID, verifying your delivery address, and adding your mobile phone number. Having accurate information on file allows our team to process your order quickly and contact you if we need assistance locating your address.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Browse the Menu and Add to Cart

    Open the delivery menu and explore what's currently available. Every item reflects real-time stock, so what you see is what you can get. Take your time, make your selections, and give your cart a final review before heading to checkout.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Submit Payment

    Once you've selected your products, choose your preferred payment method — either Pay by Bank or Pay by Text. To avoid any delays, please ensure payment is completed before checking out, as only fully paid orders are eligible for delivery dispatch.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Expect Your Driver in About an Hour

    Once we’ve confirmed your order, a driver is on the move. Most deliveries arrive in approximately one hour. Your driver will contact you upon arrival, verify your ID, and complete the handoff. That’s all there is to it.

Galloping Hill Golf Course

Galloping Hill Golf Course covers 271 acres in Kenilworth and is widely regarded as one of the most challenging public courses in the region. The 18-hole layout underwent a major renovation in 2014 by renowned architect Rees Jones, restoring the course's original shot values and updating drainage throughout. The on-site Learning Center features 52 stalls and a 9-hole practice course, making it a destination for players of all skill levels.

Black Brook Park

Black Brook Park on N 19th Street is one of 36 Union County parks and a well-used outdoor resource in Kenilworth. The park features baseball, soccer, and softball fields, along with fishing access, and the Black Brook Park Path connects directly to the East Coast Greenway, the long-distance trail network stretching from Maine to Florida. It's a solid everyday green space with recreational depth that goes beyond what its footprint might suggest.

The Kenilworth Diner has been serving the borough for over 40 years with fresh, homestyle cooking and the kind of attentive service that turns first-time visitors into regulars. The menu covers fresh soups, hearty entrees, and an award-winning cheesecake that has earned a following well beyond Kenilworth's borders. Ava's Kitchen and Bar

Tucked just off Kenilworth Boulevard, Ava's Kitchen and Bar is a neighborhood spot built around a wood-burning brick oven and a genuine commitment to the craft of pizza-making. The kitchen uses sourdough starters, lengthy fermentations, stone-ground local flours, and seasonal ingredients to produce thin, crackly-crusted pies with a flavor profile that's hard to replicate. Beyond pizza, the bar offers 32 beers on tap, wines by the glass, and an extensive selection of craft spirits and cocktails.

Visit06

Oswald J. Nitschke House

The Oswald J. Nitschke House is an 1880 wood-framed farmhouse and one of Kenilworth's most significant historic landmarks. Built by a German immigrant who served three terms as mayor and championed the borough's incorporation in 1907, the house was saved from demolition in 2003 when the Kenilworth Historical Society relocated it to prevent its loss to commercial development. Preserved and restored with support from the New Jersey Historic Trust, it serves as a living history museum and cultural arts center that reflects the immigrant influences and suburban development that shaped the borough's early character.

Kenilworth Weed Delivery FAQ

What’s the age requirement to receive cannabis delivery in Kenilworth?

In New Jersey, the law sets the adult-use minimum at 21, and that applies to every Lady L delivery order without exception. Your age is verified during account registration, and the driver will confirm your ID again upon arrival.

What ID do I need to show when my Lady L delivery arrives?

Government-issued photo ID is the standard across the board. Driver's licenses, state-issued identification cards, and passports all qualify, and unexpired out-of-state IDs are accepted without issue. You'll need to upload your ID to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and have the physical document ready when your driver shows up.

Does Lady L require a medical cannabis card for delivery?

Not at all. Lady L is a fully adult-use operation, open to any customer 21 or older. No referral, no card, no special status required. A confirmed account and a valid ID are the only things standing between you and a delivery order.

How much can I order in a single Kenilworth delivery?

New Jersey law permits us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. Here's how that breaks down across product types:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to a Kenilworth cannabis delivery order?

Three taxes apply to adult-use cannabis in New Jersey: a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where the local municipality has authorized it. All of them are broken out at checkout before your order is confirmed.

How do I pay for a Lady L delivery order?

ACH payment and a debit option are both accepted for delivery, but cash is not. You’ll set up your preferred payment method through your account at registration, then Lady L collects full payment before confirming your order and sending a driver. Questions about the process? Call the store directly at 201-595-0706.

My delivery is taking longer than expected. What now?

One hour is the typical window for weed delivery Kenilworth customers receive from Lady L, but order volume, road conditions, and distance can all factor in on any given day. If your delivery is running behind, don't wait; reach out to our team directly. We keep tabs on every order and will give you a straight answer on timing.

What if I need to cancel or adjust my order?

The sooner you contact us, the better. Once a driver is dispatched, making changes to an order may not be possible. Call us at 201-595-0706 or reach out through the website, and our team will do whatever we can to help.

Does someone need to be available when my delivery arrives?

Yes, an adult 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID must be there when the driver arrives. No exceptions. If a qualifying adult is not available at the time of delivery, the driver can’t complete the handoff, so make sure you’re reachable during the estimated window.

Does Lady L offer deals or discounts on Kenilworth weed delivery?

Lady L offers deals every day, but our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions. It's always worth checking with our team or browsing the promotions section on the website to see what's currently active.

As for loyalty points, they apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases, which means every order is building toward something. A dollar spent is a point earned, and every 100 points converts to $5 in store credit. Lady L also runs dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip my delivery driver?

It's never expected, but it's always appreciated. A tip in the 15-20% range is a solid way to recognize a driver who showed up on time, handled the delivery with discretion, and made the whole experience feel effortless.
