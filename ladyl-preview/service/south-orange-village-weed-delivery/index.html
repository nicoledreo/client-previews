Around South Orange Village

Before or After Your Delivery Order

South Orange Village earns its reputation as a place with city sense and village vibe every single day. A walkable downtown, a thriving arts scene, and the sweeping natural landscape of the South Mountain Reservation give this Essex County gem a range that few towns its size can match. With the cannabis delivery South Orange Village residents love just a few clicks away, everything the village has to offer is yours to enjoy on your own terms.

01

South Mountain Reservation

Spanning 2,110 Olmsted-designed acres along the Watchung Mountain ridges and bordering South Orange, South Mountain Reservation is one of the most impressive natural spaces in northern New Jersey. Hiking trails, carriage roads, a dog park, a wildlife refuge, a wildflower sculpture park, scenic overlooks, and the Washington Rock Lookout Historic Site make it a destination that rewards every kind of outdoor explorer.

Visit02

South Orange Performing Arts Center

Located in the heart of downtown South Orange Village, SOPAC is a cultural anchor that draws world-class performers to an intimate and accessible venue. From music and comedy to spoken word and visual art in the Herb and Milly Iris Gallery, its programming spans genres and disciplines in a way that keeps the calendar consistently worth checking.

Visit03

Pierro Gallery

Founded in 1994 and housed in the beautifully renovated Baird Community Center, the Pierro Gallery is South Orange's community art destination. A non-commercial space presenting diverse established and emerging visual artists, it hosts up to seven exhibitions a year and has become both a local treasure and a regional draw.

Visit04

Meadowland Park

South Orange's largest and most visited park, Meadowland covers 45 acres in the heart of the village. A duck pond, the beloved Flood Hill, tennis and basketball courts, soccer and baseball fields, and the iconic Skate House give this community green space a personality that has made it a neighborhood fixture for generations.

Visit05

Bistro d’Azure

A fine dining standout on Academy Street, Bistro d'Azur brings the flavors of Southern France and the broader Mediterranean to downtown South Orange. The farm-to-table menu draws on locally sourced ingredients, the prix fixe format invites a leisurely pace, and the accolades from NJ Monthly speak for themselves.

Visit06

Sonny’s Bagels

A South Orange institution since 1971, Sonny's Bagels has built its reputation on hand-rolled, old-world style bagels made with simple ingredients and real technique. House-made specialty cream cheeses and smoked fish sourced from the same suppliers as New York City's most celebrated appetizing shops round out a menu that keeps locals coming back without question.

What's the minimum age for South Orange Village weed delivery?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law is clear on this point: you must be 21 or older to place a delivery order. Age is verified during account setup, and your driver will check your ID at the door before the handoff is completed. No exceptions apply.

What kind of ID do you accept for a delivery order?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID qualifies. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports are all accepted. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and the physical ID must be present when your driver arrives.

Is a medical cannabis card required for delivery in South Orange Village?

No medical card is needed. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are the only requirements to get your first order going.

How much can I purchase for a South Orange Village weed delivery?

New Jersey law allows us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That can include any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to a cannabis delivery order in South Orange Village?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All of these apply to delivery orders exactly as they do to in-store purchases, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.

How can I pay for a Lady L delivery order?

Delivery orders are processed through your Lady L account via ACH. Cash is not accepted for delivery orders, but a debit option is available. For any payment questions, reach out directly at 201-595-0706. Note that full payment must be submitted and captured before your order is confirmed and sent out.

What happens if my South Orange Village weed delivery is running behind?

Typical delivery time runs approximately one hour, though order volume, traffic, and distance can all play a role. If your order is taking longer than expected, get in touch with our team. We track every order closely and communicate delays as proactively as possible.

Is it possible to change or cancel my order after it's been placed?

Contact us as soon as possible if anything needs to change. Once your order has been dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but we'll do everything we can to help. Reach us by phone at 201-595-0706 or through the website.

Does someone need to be home when my delivery arrives?

Yes. An adult 21 or older must be present and ready to show a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the order can’t be completed, so make sure you're available during your estimated delivery window to keep things on track.

Are there any deals or discounts available on South Orange Village weed delivery?

Delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, but loyalty points apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases. Every dollar spent earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit.

Discount programs are available for first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several other groups. Deals rotate regularly, so reach out to the team or check the promotions section for what's currently active.

Should I tip my Lady L delivery driver?

Tipping is always optional, but it goes a long way. Our drivers work hard to make every cannabis delivery South Orange Village customers request smooth, discreet, and right on time. A tip of 15 to 20% is a genuinely appreciated way to recognize that effort.
