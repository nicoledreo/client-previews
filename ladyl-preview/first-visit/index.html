Your First Visit to Lady L

New to cannabis, or just new to us? Here's everything to know before you walk into our West Side shop, from the ID you'll need to what happens at the counter.

Before You Go

Your first visit isn't a test. You don't need to know strain names or fake your way through the lingo. Just get a few basics sorted before you leave the house.

  1. 1

    Grab your ID

    Adult-use cannabis in New Jersey is for adults 21 and older. Bring a valid, government-issued photo ID, like a driver's license or passport. You'll show it at the door and again at the register, and out-of-state IDs are welcome too.

  2. 2

    Peek at the menu

    Browse our live menu to see what's in stock today. Availability changes regularly, so checking ahead saves time and gives you something to ask about.

  3. 3

    Order ahead, if you like

    We recommend creating an account and placing your order online so it's ready for your visit. Select pickup at checkout and our team will have it ready during store hours. Last call for pickup is 1 hour before closing.

  4. 4

    Plan your stop

    We're at 547 West Side Avenue. Store hours are Monday to Thursday 10am to 9pm, Friday and Saturday 10am to 10pm, and Sunday 11am to 8pm. There's no lot, but there are metered spots right in front of the shop. More on getting here.

Rather stay in? Delivery covers Jersey City and a broad surrounding area, and it's free on orders of $75 or more. You'll need a Lady L account with your ID uploaded before ordering.

02

At the Counter

Lady L feels more like a well-loved living room than a clinic. Our budtenders know their cannabis and love sharing it in an easy-to-understand way, and first-timers can expect a calm, clear, no-pressure conversation.

Telling a budtender it's your first time is the fastest way to a good visit. A few other things worth mentioning:

  • How you'd like to feel. Relaxed, social, mellow or creative can each point toward different products.
  • How you'd like to consume. Smoking, vaping, edibles and tinctures each work on their own clock.
  • Your comfort zone. Ask for low-dose or beginner-friendly options, and mention anything you're unsure about.
  • Your budget. Sharing a number helps your budtender narrow things down.

No gatekeeping, no attitude, just real people and open doors. Ask as many questions as you like.

Words you'll hear

  • Flower: dried cannabis buds that can be smoked or vaporized.
  • Pre-roll: a ready-to-go joint.
  • Edibles: cannabis-infused foods and drinks, like gummies.
  • Tincture: a liquid you place under your tongue.
  • THC and CBD: THC is the compound responsible for the high; CBD is a non-intoxicating compound many people use to keep things gentle.

Want more? Our cannabis glossary breaks down dozens of terms in plain language.

03

Paying at the Register

We accept cash and debit card only. Debit is processed as a “cashless ATM” transaction: the POS takes an amount higher than your receipt total and gives you the difference back in cash. There is a $3.50 ATM fee.

Why the workaround? Traditional credit card networks like Visa and Mastercard don't allow cannabis transactions, because cannabis is still federally illegal. It's not ideal, but until federal laws change, it's one of the few digital payment options available.

While you're at the register, join Lady L Rewards. It's free: sign up with your name and mobile number or email at checkout, and you'll earn 1 point for every dollar spent (100 points = $5).

First-timer perk: a new customer discount is part of our rotating deals. Offers change, so ask your budtender what's active when you visit.

04

How Much You Can Buy

There's a cap on every purchase. In a single transaction, adults 21+ can buy up to the equivalent of 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of usable cannabis, which includes:

  • 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of dried flower
  • 4 grams of cannabis concentrates
  • 4 grams of oil formulated for vape cartridges
  • 1,000 mg of ingestible cannabis-infused products (edibles)

You can combine products, as long as the total stays within the equivalent of 1 ounce per transaction. More in our New Jersey cannabis laws guide.

05

After Your Visit

Start low, go slow

Begin with a small amount, give it time to fully kick in, and only add more once you know how the first amount feels. Inhaled cannabis usually takes effect within a few minutes. Edibles work on a much slower clock, often thirty minutes to two hours, so wait at least two hours before deciding whether to take more. Everyone's different, and our edibles dosing guide walks through it.

Set the scene

Pick a free afternoon or evening with nothing pressing, keep water and snacks within reach, and settle in somewhere calm and familiar. Avoid combining THC with alcohol or other intoxicating substances.

Store it safely

Keep flower, vapes, gummies, drinks and infused snacks locked away from children and pets, in their original labeled packaging.

Don't drive

Do not drive, use machinery or bike in traffic after using cannabis. Plan to enjoy it at home: New Jersey permits consumption in private residences, not in public spaces or in vehicles.

If it's too much

Stay calm, move somewhere quiet and comfortable, skip anything else intoxicating, and seek medical help if you need it. Next time, simply dose a touch lower.

First-Visit FAQ

Do I need a medical card?

No. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary, so any customer 21 or older with a valid ID can shop. More on medical vs. adult-use cannabis.

Can I bring a friend?

Of course, as long as they're 21 or older and bring a valid ID too. Everyone must be 21 or older to enter the shop.

Is it okay to just browse?

Absolutely. Browse our shelves, discover in-shop surprises and ask our budtenders anything. There's no pressure to buy.

Do first-time customers get a discount?

First-time customer discounts are part of our rotating promotion mix. Since offers can change, ask the team what's currently active when you visit or browse online.
