Lavender Lemon Spritz

Floral, tart and fizzy, this alcohol-free spritz is our at-home nod to the mocktail bar from our grand opening. Pour it as is, or try the optional infused glass if you're 21+.

Recipe card

Lavender Lemon Spritz

  • Yield 1 spritz (the syrup makes enough for about 6)
  • Time 15 minutes, plus about 1 hour to steep and chill the syrup

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Make the syrup: warm the water and sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves and the mix just simmers.

  2. 2

    Take the pan off the heat, stir in the lavender, cover and steep for 10 minutes. Taste, and steep a few minutes longer only if you want it stronger.

  3. 3

    Strain into a clean jar and chill until cold.

  4. 4

    Fill a tall glass with ice. Add the lavender syrup and lemon juice and stir.

  5. 5

    Top with sparkling water and give it one gentle stir to keep the bubbles lively.

  6. 6

    Garnish with a lemon wheel and a lavender sprig. Taste and adjust with more lemon or syrup.

21+

Start low, go slow. For adults 21+. Keep out of reach of children. May pose health risks, especially for pregnant or breastfeeding women. Avoid driving or heavy machinery use.

Dosing guide

Inspired by the Mocktail Bar

When Lady L opened its doors at 547 West Side Ave in Jersey City, the grand opening lineup included the Hydrate & Elevate Mocktail Bar, pouring THC-infused Lavender Lemonade. This spritz brings that flavor combo home: lavender syrup for the floral, fresh lemon for the zing and sparkling water for the fizz. 🍋

The base recipe is alcohol-free on purpose. Our own safety advice is to avoid combining THC with alcohol, so this is a drink every grown-up at the table can enjoy, whether they partake or not. Fun fact from our trichomes guide: lavender grows trichomes too.

Spritz Smarter

  • Use culinary lavender. Buy dried lavender sold for cooking, not decorative bundles.
  • Don't over-steep. Lavender turns soapy fast, so start at 10 minutes and taste.
  • Make it ahead. The syrup keeps in a sealed jar in the fridge for about two weeks.
  • Like it tart? Use 1 tablespoon of syrup and an extra squeeze of lemon.
  • Swap the bubbles. Plain seltzer, club soda or a lemon sparkling water all work.
  • Hosting? For a non-infused pitcher, stir the whole batch of syrup with 3/4 cup lemon juice and top with about 1 liter of sparkling water right before serving.

Make It Infused (Optional)

For adults 21+ only. Lady L carries occasional beverage options, depending on availability, so check the live menu or ask a budtender what's in stock. To infuse one glass, build the spritz as above and pour in one single-serve cannabis beverage in place of the sparkling water.

  • One per glass, never more. The dose on the label is the dose in your glass, so read it before you pour.
  • Sip slowly, then wait. Like edibles, drinks take longer to kick in because your body needs to digest them first. Wait at least two hours before deciding on another.
  • Never infuse a pitcher. Infused drinks go in individual glasses only, so nobody gets a dose they didn't choose.
  • Make it easy to tell apart. Serve infused spritzes in a different glass, and only to adults 21+ who asked for one.
  • Keep it booze-free. Never add alcohol to an infused spritz, and never drive after drinking one.
  • Lock up the rest. Keep any unopened cannabis drinks, and the rest of your stash, locked away from children and pets.
New to cannabis drinks? Start with the classic spritz and chat with a budtender about what fits your comfort level. Everyone's different, and there's no rush.
