A cause we champion

Traffic Reform Warriors

Traffic safety shouldn’t be an afterthought—it should be a priority.

The Traffic Reform Warriors are a coalition of Jersey City residents fighting for safer streets, better enforcement, and real accountability from city officials.

We are parents, commuters, cyclists, and pedestrians who believe that no one should fear for their life just crossing the street.
