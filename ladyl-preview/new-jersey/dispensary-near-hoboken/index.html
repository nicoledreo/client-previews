A Day Worth Having: Explore Hoboken, NJ

Hoboken is one of New Jersey's most walkable, most vibrant small cities. Before you make the short drive to our dispensary near Hoboken, or after you're done with us, the city has plenty worth staying for. Here are a few of our favorite spots.

01

Hudson River Waterfront Walkway

The Hoboken stretch of the waterfront walkway is one of the best free experiences in Hudson County. You get unobstructed Manhattan skyline views, fresh air, and a flat path that connects several parks along the water. Walk it at golden hour and you'll understand why people move here from the city. It's photo-worthy in every season and a perfect way to clear your head before heading our way.

Visit02

Gravity Vault Hoboken

Gravity Vault is Hoboken's indoor rock climbing gym, and it's genuinely fun for all skill levels. Go for a morning session before your visit, or bring friends for an afternoon of something active and hands-on. The routes challenge you without intimidating you. The energy inside is welcoming and community-focused, which makes it a natural complement to the kind of experience we aim to deliver at Lady L.

Visit03

Maxwell Place Park

Maxwell Place Park is right on the Hudson waterfront and offers some of the best open green space in the city. There's a playground, open lawn, and stunning water views just steps from the residential neighborhood. People who just want to sit and breathe find something here. Check the Hoboken parks schedule for seasonal events and programming throughout the year.

Visit04

Hudson Table

Hudson Table is a culinary event space that brings people together around food. It hosts hands-on cooking classes, private experiences, and team events in a warm, modern setting. If you're planning a day in Hoboken, check their calendar for upcoming classes before you arrive. It's a social, creative stop that draws curious, community-minded locals. The vibe here rhymes with ours.

Visit05

Yoga Renew Hoboken

Yoga Renew offers classes and teacher training in a space that doesn't take itself too seriously. It's accessible, welcoming, and built for people at all experience levels. A morning class here followed by a drive to Lady L makes for a legitimately good day. Both spaces share a commitment to meeting people where they are and helping them feel better when they leave.

Visit06

Carlos' Bakery

Carlos' Bakery is a Hoboken landmark. Known from the TV show Cake Boss, this spot has been serving the community for decades with cakes, pastries, and specialty items that are just as good as the reputation suggests. Pick something up before your drive to Lady L, or swing by on the way back. Either way, it's a classic Hoboken experience you don't want to skip.

Visit07

Napoli's Hoboken Pizza

Hoboken has a serious pizza reputation, and Napoli's holds a strong place in that legacy. This neighborhood spot is casual, reliable, and exactly what you want after a full afternoon of errands. The slices speak for themselves. Stop in before the drive, or loop back after your visit with us for a well-earned meal.

Willie McBride's is one of Hoboken's most beloved bars. It's a classic Irish pub with a warm, lived-in atmosphere, strong pours, and a crowd that feels like regulars even on a first visit. Cannabis Guide for Hoboken Dispensary Shoppers

What makes Lady L different from other dispensaries near Hoboken?

Lady L is a woman-owned cannabis microbusiness built by a real Jersey City community member. Founder Lili Hollandt designed this shop to feel like a home, not a store. Our budtenders are warm, knowledgeable, and judgment-free. The product selection is curated, not inflated. In 2025, Lady L earned recognition as the Best Dispensary in Jersey City by the Local Girl audience. Hoboken dispensary shoppers who make the trip keep coming back because the service is personal and the vibe is one-of-a-kind.

Where is Lady L Cannabis located?

We are at 547 West Side Avenue, Jersey City, NJ 07304, on the West Side's main corridor between Clendenny Avenue and Oxford Avenue. From Hoboken, the fastest route is via I-78 W, which takes about 22 minutes and 6 miles. This route includes tolls. Street parking is available on West Side Avenue and the surrounding side streets.

How old do I need to be to visit a dispensary near Hoboken?

You must be 21 or older to enter Lady L and make any adult-use cannabis purchase. Bring a valid, government-issued photo ID every time you visit. We accept all valid government-issued IDs, including out-of-state licenses. Temporary or expired IDs are not accepted. There are no exceptions.

Do I need to show ID to purchase cannabis?

Yes, every single time. A valid government-issued photo ID is required for every visit. That means a driver's license or passport. Temporary IDs and expired IDs won't work. This applies to both in-store shopping and delivery orders.

Do I need a medical card to shop at Lady L?

No medical card required. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary near Hoboken. Any customer 21 or older can walk in and make a purchase. Your valid photo ID is all you need.

How much cannabis can I legally buy near Hoboken, NJ?

Dispensaries are allowed to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. This can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, such as purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis purchases in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey are subject to a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All taxes are applied at checkout. Our team can walk you through the breakdown if you have questions.

What forms of payment do you accept at Lady L?

We accept cash. Credit cards are not accepted for in-store purchases. All delivery orders require full payment before the order is placed. An on-site ATM is available if needed.

Where can I legally consume cannabis near Hoboken?

Cannabis consumption is permitted in private residences in New Jersey. Public spaces, vehicles, parks, and federal property are all off-limits. You're responsible for consuming only in legal locations. Lady L doesn't offer on-site consumption space at our dispensary near Hoboken.

Does Lady L offer deals and discounts for Hoboken dispensary shoppers?

We do. Our rotating promotions include first-time customer discounts, veteran and military discounts, senior discounts, birthday discounts, referral rewards, and industry discounts. Our community VIP program, the West Side Connection Discount, gives back to the people who are part of our neighborhood. Through our loyalty program, you earn one point for every dollar spent. Every 100 points is worth $5 in store credit. Points apply to both in-store and delivery purchases. Check with our team for what's running when you arrive.

Ready to Go? Directions to Our Dispensary Near Hoboken

We are easy to reach from Hoboken no matter how you travel. Here's what each option looks like.

Driving from Hoboken

We’re an easy drive over:

  • Via I-78 W (~22 min, 6.0 miles) — The fastest route from Hoboken into Jersey City's West Side. Note: this route has tolls.
  • Via via US-1 Truck S/US-9 Truck S (~25 min, 4.2 miles) — A toll-free alternative through Jersey City surface streets, avoiding the NJ-440 S slowdown.

Public Transit from Hoboken

Taking transit? Hoboken is well-connected, and a few options get you close to us.

  • HBLR + Bus 1 (~38 min): Take the Hudson-Bergen Light Rail from Hoboken, connect to Bus 1, and walk about 6 minutes to us.
  • PATH + Bus 1 (~37 min): Take the PATH from Hoboken, walk to Bus 1, and ride into the West Side. The slightly faster of the two options.

Biking from Hoboken

The ride from Hoboken to Lady L is mostly flat and covers about 3.8 to 4.4 miles. All routes are manageable.
