Cannabis Guide for Dispensary Shoppers

What makes Lady L different from other dispensaries near Newark International Airport?

The dispensary near Newark International Airport that travelers keep coming back to is Lady L. This is a woman-owned microbusiness where the founder is genuinely present, the budtenders know their products, and the shop feels nothing like a corporate cannabis chain. Lili Hollandt built Lady L from scratch on Jersey City's West Side, and in 2025, the Local Girl audience named it the Best Dispensary in Jersey City. The 15-minute drive from EWR is one of the shortest on this page, and the experience on the other end makes it worth every minute.

Where is Lady L Cannabis located?

Our shop is at 547 West Side Avenue, Jersey City, NJ 07304, on the West Side's main corridor between Clendenny Avenue and Lexington Avenue. From Newark Airport, take US-1 Truck N/US-9 Truck N north to Communipaw Ave in Jersey City, then turn right onto West Side Ave. The trip covers 7.7 miles and takes about 15 minutes. Street parking is available on West Side Avenue and nearby side streets.

How old do I need to be to visit a dispensary near Newark International Airport?

You need to be 21 or older to shop at Lady L. Adult-use cannabis purchases require a valid, government-issued photo ID on every visit. We accept all valid government-issued IDs, including those from other states and international visitors. Expired and temporary IDs are never accepted.

Do I need to show ID to purchase cannabis?

Every time, no exceptions. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport. Expired IDs and temporary IDs are not accepted, whether you're shopping in-store or placing a delivery order.

Do I need a medical card to shop at Lady L?

Not at all. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary. If you're 21 or older with a valid photo ID, you're ready to shop. No medical card, no extra paperwork required.

Can I take cannabis on a plane from Newark Airport?

No. Transporting cannabis across state lines or onto a commercial flight is illegal under federal law, regardless of the laws in your destination state. Newark Liberty International Airport is a federally regulated facility, and TSA officers are required to report cannabis discoveries to law enforcement. Purchase what you plan to consume in New Jersey, and do not attempt to bring any cannabis products through security or onto a plane. Lady L is here for your New Jersey experience, not your carry-on.

How much cannabis can I legally buy?

Dispensaries are allowed to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. This can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, such as purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis purchases in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey are subject to a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All taxes are applied at checkout. Our team can walk you through the breakdown if you have questions.

What forms of payment do you accept at Lady L?

We accept cash, debit cards, pay by bank (ACH), pay by text (Apple/Google debit or credit), and Aeropay. All delivery orders require full payment (pay by bank) before the order is placed. An on-site ATM is available if needed.

Where can I legally consume cannabis near Newark International Airport?

New Jersey permits cannabis consumption in private residences. Public spaces, parks, vehicles, and federal property are all off-limits. Newark Liberty International Airport is federal property, so consumption anywhere on airport grounds is not permitted. Plan to consume at your destination once you're settled in a private space. Lady L does not have on-site consumption space.

Does Lady L offer deals and discounts for dispensary shoppers?

We do. Our rotating promotions include first-time customer discounts, veteran and military discounts, senior discounts, birthday discounts, referral rewards, and industry discounts. Our community VIP program, the West Side Connection Discount, gives back to the people who are part of our neighborhood. Through our loyalty program, you earn one point for every dollar spent. Every 100 points is worth $5 in store credit. Points apply to both in-store and delivery purchases. Check with our team for what's running when you arrive.
