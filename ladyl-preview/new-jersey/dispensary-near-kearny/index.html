How old do I need to be to visit a dispensary near Kearny?

You need to be 21 or older to shop at Lady L. Adult-use cannabis purchases require a valid, government-issued photo ID on every visit. We accept all valid government-issued IDs, including those from other states. Expired and temporary IDs are never accepted.

Do I need to show ID to purchase cannabis?

Every time, no exceptions. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport. Expired IDs and temporary IDs are not accepted, whether you're shopping in-store or placing a delivery order.

Do I need a medical card to shop at Lady L?

Not at all. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary. If you're 21 or older with a valid photo ID, you're ready to shop. No medical card, no extra paperwork required.

How much cannabis can I legally buy near Kearny, NJ?

Dispensaries are allowed to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. This can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, such as purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis purchases in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey are subject to a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All taxes are applied at checkout. Our team can walk you through the breakdown if you have questions.

What forms of payment do you accept at Lady L?

We accept cash, debit cards, pay by bank (ACH), pay by text (Apple/Google debit or credit), and Aeropay. All delivery orders require full payment (pay by bank) before the order is placed. An on-site ATM is available if needed.

Where can I legally consume cannabis near Kearny?

New Jersey permits cannabis consumption in private residences. Public spaces, parks, vehicles, and federal property are all off-limits. You're responsible for knowing and following the rules wherever you consume. Lady L does not have on-site consumption space.
