NJ cannabis laws

Your first-visit checklist

Before You Come In

  • Your ID. You must be 21 or older to enter, so bring a valid, government-issued photo ID every time.
  • Cash or debit. That's what we take (details in the FAQ below).
  • Your order, if you like. We recommend creating an account and placing your order online so it's ready for your visit.
  • Your questions. Our experienced budtenders are ready to point you to a product that's just right for you.

Purchase limits

Adults 21+ can purchase up to the equivalent of 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of usable cannabis per transaction. That includes:

  • 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of dried flower
  • 4 grams of cannabis concentrates
  • 4 grams of oil formulated for vape cartridges
  • 1,000 mg of ingestible cannabis-infused products (edibles)

You can combine products, as long as the total doesn't exceed the equivalent of 1 ounce per transaction. New to all this? Our first-visit guide walks you through it.

Store FAQ

Jersey City Store FAQ

What do I need to bring with me to shop at the store?

You'll need a photo ID to verify that you're 21 or older. We accept all valid government-issued IDs, including out-of-state IDs.

Can I place an order for pickup?

Yes, we absolutely recommend you browse our menu and place an order before coming by. Last call for pickup is 1 hour before closing.

Do you accept cash or cards?

We accept cash and debit card only. Debit card transactions are processed using a “cashless ATM,” where our POS takes an amount higher than your receipt total and gives you the difference back in cash. There is a $3.50 ATM fee. Our FAQ explains why.

Is there parking at the store?

There's no lot, but there are metered spots right in front of the shop on West Side Ave.

Do I need a medical card to shop at Lady L?

No medical card is required. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, so any customer 21 or older can walk in and make a purchase. Learn more about medical vs. adult-use cannabis in New Jersey.

Do you offer cannabis delivery in Jersey City?

Yes. Delivery covers Jersey City and a broad surrounding area of northern New Jersey, and it's free on orders of $75 or more. You'll need a Lady L account with your ID uploaded and payment (Pay by Bank or Pay by Text) completed in full before checkout. A valid ID is also required upon delivery.

Can I consume cannabis at the shop?

No. Lady L does not provide on-site consumption space. Cannabis consumption is permitted in private residences in New Jersey, and it's your responsibility to consume only in legal locations.
