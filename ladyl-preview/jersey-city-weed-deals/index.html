Pickup · Delivery

Daily Cannabis Specials with Pickup & Delivery Convenience

Jersey City weed deals work best when they’re easy to access. Lady L offers both pickup and delivery, which makes your Jersey City weed deals easier to access, however you prefer to shop.

Pickup

Pickup is easy! Simply place your order online, select "pickup" at checkout, and come see us at our dispensary on the designated pickup day. Our team will have your order ready for pickup during our business hours.

Delivery

Delivery covers Jersey City and a broad surrounding area across northern New Jersey through two service zones. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more, with a $75 minimum for delivery. Loyalty points still apply, and delivery deals may differ from what’s available in-store.

Before you order

If you choose delivery, you’ll need a Lady L account, a valid government-issued ID uploaded before ordering, and payment submitted in full before the order is placed. A valid government-issued ID is also required upon delivery. For shoppers who want a faster in-person experience, pickup is a strong option that keeps things simple while still letting you order ahead.
