The scoop

We’re bringing the Lady L vibes to Jersey City Pride Festival on Saturday, August 23rd. We can’t wait to meet new friends and hopefully see you out there!

Lady L was created for customers who aren’t always seen or represented in traditional cannabis spaces. That means we’ve built our shop to feel welcoming, affirming, and comfortable for everyone. Pride is the perfect time to celebrate that together.

Swing by our booth to:

  • Meet the Lady L team & chat with your West Side neighbors
  • Snag free swag & merch (while it lasts!)
  • Score exclusive specials for new loyalty members
  • Send your friends & Pride Fam our way for a warm welcome and extra perks

Mark your calendar, grab your crew, and come celebrate Pride with us!
