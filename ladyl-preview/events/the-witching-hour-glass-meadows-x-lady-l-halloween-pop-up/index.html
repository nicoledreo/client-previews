The scoop

Something magical is brewing on the West Side!

Lady L welcomes Glass Meadows for a special Halloween pop-up featuring the highly anticipated return of @climatewitch, back by popular demand for an evening of tarot readings, cosmic curiosity, and Halloween fun.

What’s Happening?

Tarot with @climatewitch

The cards are calling! Our favorite climate witch returns to Lady L for another evening of mystical insights and playful predictions. Readings are subject to availability.

Halloween Costume Contest

Come dressed to impress! Whether your Halloween personality is spooky, glamorous, mysterious, or wonderfully bizarre, bring your best look and join the fun.

Glass Meadows Pop-Up

Meet Glass Meadows at Lady L for a special Halloween appearance and learn more about the featured brand.

Community, Costumes & Cosmic Energy

Celebrate Halloween with your West Side neighbors and the Lady L community.
