Shop by strain type

Indica, Hybrid and Sativa are a helpful starting point for understanding how different strains might make you feel. Our shorthand: Indica for relaxation and all the feelings related to being stoned, Hybrid for the fun, groovy times between relaxation and high vibration, and Sativa for energy, activity and higher vibrational adventures. Labels are only part of the story, and everyone's different.

Pickup · Delivery

Pickup or delivery?

Pickup on West Side Ave

Order ahead on the live menu, choose pickup at checkout, then swing by 547 West Side Avenue with a photo ID showing you're 21+. We take cash and debit card only, and debit runs as a cashless ATM transaction with a $3.50 ATM fee. Last call for pickup is 1 hour before closing.

Store hoursStore details

Delivery to your door

Create a Lady L account and upload a valid government-issued ID before you order, then complete payment in full with Pay by Bank or Pay by Text before checkout. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more, and typical delivery time is about an hour.

Delivery areas

We deliver across Jersey City and a broad surrounding area of northern New Jersey through two service zones. Hours and directions live on our Jersey City store page, and our delivery areas page lists the towns we serve.

Shopping FAQ

Still curious?

Ask a budtender in store at 547 West Side Avenue, or give the shop a call.

Can I place an order for pickup?

Yes, we absolutely recommend you browse our menu and place an order before coming by.

Do I need a medical card to shop at Lady L?

Not at all. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary. If you're 21 or older with a valid photo ID, you're ready to shop. No medical card, no extra paperwork required.

Can I ask budtenders for recommendations?

Yes, please do. Our experienced retail staff is ready to point you to a product just right for you.
