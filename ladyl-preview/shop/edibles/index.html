The basics

Edibles, explained

Edibles are cannabis-infused foods and drinks, like gummies and chocolates. Because your body digests them first, they take longer to kick in and tend to last for hours. Along the way, your liver converts THC into a more potent compound called 11-hydroxy-THC, which is why an edible can feel stronger than the same amount smoked.

On our shelves

What's in our edibles case

Our edibles range covers gummies, chocolates, and other ingestible options across a variety of strengths, flavors, and textures. Sleep gummies are especially popular with our regulars.

Brands we've championed include Wana (we wrote a whole love letter) and Wyld, with more on our brand wall. Shopping for edibles in Jersey City? Availability rotates and freshness matters, so the live edibles menu is the most accurate look at what's in stock.

Step by step

Your first bite, step by step

  1. 1

    Read the label

    For edibles, milligrams matter more than percentages. Check the THC per piece and the total in the package.

  2. 2

    Start small

    Cutting a gummy in half, or even into quarters, is a completely normal way to start. Our dosing guide has a starting chart by tolerance.

  3. 3

    Wait it out

    Effects typically begin within thirty minutes to two hours. Wait at least two hours before deciding on more.

  4. 4

    Clear your evening

    Once effects begin, most people feel them for four to eight hours. Stay somewhere comfy with water nearby, skip the alcohol and don't drive.

  5. 5

    Ask at the counter

    “How much should I take?” and “Which one should I try first?” are two of the most common questions we hear. Ask away: that's what our budtenders are here for.

Store them like a grown-up: edibles can look like everyday snacks, so keep them in their original packaging and locked away from kids and pets.

Edibles FAQ

How many edibles can I buy at once?

Adults 21+ can buy up to 1,000 mg of ingestible cannabis-infused products per transaction, as part of the 1-ounce-equivalent limit. See the full list on our FAQ.

General information, not legal advice. For the latest rules, visit the New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory Commission.

I don't feel anything yet. Can I take more?

Not yet. Taking a second dose too early is the most common cause of uncomfortable experiences, because the first one hasn't finished kicking in. Set a timer for two hours and really wait it out.

Do edibles hit harder on an empty stomach?

Generally, yes. An empty stomach can bring effects on faster and harder than expected. A light snack beforehand, even crackers or toast, can make the onset feel more gradual.

What's microdosing?

Taking very small amounts of THC for subtle effects. Edibles made with smaller servings make it easier than slicing a standard piece into uneven bits. Our dosing guide covers it.
