Know your options

Gear that makes it easy

Accessories are everything around the cannabis itself: the tools that grind, store, carry and power your favorites. Our accessories section rotates like the rest of the menu, so peek at the live accessories menu before you stop by.

Grinders

Break flower up evenly for a smoother roll or pack. A grinder with a screened chamber also catches kief, that golden dust of trichomes.

Storage

Airtight, cool and dark is how flower keeps its frost and aroma. A container that locks is even better if kids or pets live with you.

Batteries

Most vape carts screw onto a standard battery. Bring your cart or ask a budtender to double-check the fit.

Rolling gear

Papers and cones turn ground flower into a joint. Short on time? A ready-made pre-roll skips that step.

The short version

Grind like you mean it

Our Trichomes 101 guide makes a strong case for grinding gently. The short version:

  • Grind right before you use it. A coarser grind keeps more of the trichome structure intact.
  • Skip the ultra-fine powder. It knocks trichome heads loose and speeds up terpene loss.
  • Store buds airtight in a cool, dark place, and don't shake the jar or squeeze the buds.
  • Kief from your grinder's chamber is potent, so go easy if you sprinkle it over flower.

Good to know

Shopping for cannabis accessories in Jersey City? Lady L is a 21+ shop, so bring a photo ID that shows you're 21 or older, whether you're buying flower or accessories. In store we take cash and debit only.

Pickup

Accessories have their own section on the live menu. For pickup, select it at checkout and swing by 547 West Side Ave; last call is 1 hour before closing. Coming from Journal Square, Historic Downtown, Greenville or The Heights? We're a quick trip away.

Delivery

Ordering cannabis for delivery? You'll need a Lady L account, an uploaded ID and payment in full before ordering, and delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. Typical delivery time is about an hour.

What accessories do you carry?

It rotates. The accessories section of the live menu shows what's in right now, and our budtenders can tell you what's on the shelf when you stop by.

Can I return an accessory?

Per our terms, eligible non-cannabis items, such as merchandise, may be returned within 7 days of purchase if unused, in original packaging and with proof of purchase. Refunds, if applicable, go back to the original payment method. Cannabis products can't be returned.

Do I need to be 21+ to shop for accessories?

Yes. Everyone shopping at Lady L needs a valid government-issued photo ID showing they're 21 or older, and out-of-state IDs are welcome.

Which battery fits my cart?

Most cartridges screw onto a standard vape battery. If you're unsure, bring your cart along and ask a budtender. More on carts on our vapes page.
