In our words

Be your higher self

Here's how we'd start that conversation at our Jersey City shop. Think of these vibes as starting points, not promises: the same product can feel different from one person to the next, and even different for the same person on a different day.

Five places to start

Pick your vibe

Wind down

Couch nights, slow Sundays, a long bath. Many people reach for Indica-leaning flower when they want to settle in. Our shorthand for Indica: relaxation and all the feelings related to being stoned.

Shop indica flower

Social & groovy

Game night, dinner with friends, the good playlist. We file Hybrids under all the fun, groovy, joyful times between relaxation and high vibration.

Shop hybrid flower

Daytime adventure

Cleaning day with a soundtrack, a walk with the dog, that project you keep putting off. Sativa-leaning strains are the ones many people describe as energizing: think energy, activity and higher vibrational adventures.

Shop sativa flower

Creative flow

Sketchbooks, playlists, side projects. Some people like a Sativa-leaning or Hybrid strain here, and a vape's quick onset makes it easy to take one small puff and check in with yourself before deciding on more.

Explore vapes

Low & slow

Edibles take their time, often 30 minutes to two hours to kick in, and they last longer than smoking or vaping. Start with a small amount and wait at least two hours before taking more.

Explore edibles

Want the details behind low and slow? Our guide on how to dose edibles walks you through it, step by step.

Quick tips

Know what you're shopping

A few things shape the vibe more than the name on the jar:

  • 01

    THC isn't a scoreboard. It's the main intoxicating compound in cannabis, but higher isn't automatically better. What is THC? breaks it down.

  • 02

    Aroma counts. Terpenes, the aromatic compounds made in a flower's trichomes, give each strain its scent and influence how a product feels. Meet the frost in Trichomes 101.

  • 03

    Strain labels are a starting point. Most modern cannabis no longer fits neatly into Indica, Sativa or Hybrid, so read the label as a hint, not a rule.

  • 04

    Format changes the clock. Inhaled cannabis usually takes effect within a few minutes, while edibles work on a much slower clock. New to it all? Start with our no-pressure beginner's guide.

A friendly reminder

Everyone's different

Your body, your tolerance, your mood and your setting all play a role. Start low, go slow, and ask a budtender. Tell us how you want to feel, whether it's your first time and any concerns you have, and we'll steer you toward gentle options. For adults 21+ only, and never drive high.

Will a strain type always feel the same way?

No. Indica, Sativa and Hybrid are a helpful starting point, but a product's actual cannabinoid and terpene content shapes your experience far more than the category name on the jar. Your dose and your setting matter too.

Is a higher THC number better?

Not automatically. The amount you actually take, your tolerance, the format, terpenes, freshness and even your mood all play a part. Start with a manageable amount and pay attention to how you feel.

What should I tell a budtender?

Keep it honest and simple: whether it's your first time, how you want to feel, any concerns you have, and whether you'd like low-dose or beginner-friendly formats. No budtender will judge you.

Can I shop by vibe for pickup or delivery?

Yes. Order on our live menu for pickup at 547 West Side Avenue, or choose delivery, which is free on orders of $75 or more and typically arrives in about an hour.
