Know your options

Vapes, explained

A vape heats cannabis oil into vapor instead of burning it. Many people find that smoother and more discreet than smoking flower, with less smoke and odor. Effects come on fast, much like smoking, so it's easy to check in with yourself between puffs.

Cartridges

A prefilled container of cannabis oil that screws onto a standard vape battery. When it runs dry, swap in a new one.

Disposables

Battery and oil in one ready-to-go device. No setup, no matching parts: use it until it's empty.

Batteries

Carts need a compatible battery. Not sure yours fits? Ask a budtender to check.

Vape oils are often labeled Indica-leaning, Sativa-leaning or Hybrid to hint at their typical effects. It's a helpful starting point, not a promise, because everyone's different. Our cannabis guide breaks it down.

Step by step

Finding your first vape

  1. 1

    Pick your format

    Want zero setup? Go disposable. Already own a battery? A cart is your move.

  2. 2

    Read the box

    Full-spectrum, live resin, rosin, distillate: our glossary explains every word in plain language.

  3. 3

    Look past the big number

    A high THC percentage doesn't tell you how much you'll actually take in. Puff size and your own tolerance matter just as much.

  4. 4

    One small puff, then pause

    Inhaled cannabis usually kicks in within a few minutes. Take one small puff and see how you feel before you go again.

  5. 5

    Tell us your vibe

    Relaxed, social, creative? Our budtenders will point you to a match, with zero judgment and no pressure.

How much vape oil can I buy at once?

Adults 21+ can buy up to the equivalent of 1 ounce of usable cannabis per transaction. That includes up to 4 grams of oil formularized for vape cartridges. The full list lives on our FAQ.

General information, not legal advice. For the latest rules, visit the New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory Commission.

Cart or disposable: which is better for a first-timer?

Neither is better; it comes down to convenience. Disposables work right out of the box, while carts need a battery but let you switch things up. Either way, start with one small puff.

What if my vape doesn't work?

We don't accept returns or exchanges on cannabis products, due to their nature and state regulations. If you receive an incorrect or damaged item, contact us within 24 hours of purchase at (201) 595-0706 and we'll work to make it right. Details are in our terms.

Can I get vapes delivered?

Yes. Vapes are on our delivery menu, which covers Jersey City and a broad stretch of northern New Jersey through two service zones. An adult 21+ with a valid government-issued ID has to be there for the handoff. Find your town on our delivery areas page.
